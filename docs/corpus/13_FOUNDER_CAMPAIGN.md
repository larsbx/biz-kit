# 13_FOUNDER_CAMPAIGN.md — Recruiting the Demo-and-Sell Cohort

Register: business/system terms (01). Concern: the campaign recruiting founding chapter members whose role is to demonstrate and sell. Imports: funnel (12), harness (11), sponsorship/admission (04 §2), pitch discipline (12 §3), anti-relocation (00 Art. IV.4).

---

## 0. Spine rule

```
recruit(founding) = sponsors-with-standing, not customers
comp(m) = f(patronage(m), shares(m)·ΔNAV, wage(m)) ⊥ |recruits(m)|     — anti-pyramid, structural
demo(m) = m's own books on the platform                                  — the demo is personal proof
sell    = invite = sponsorship = admission event                          — growth ≡ vetting
```
A founding member sells because the system demonstrably pays *them* — routed-volume patronage, returned hours, accruing stake — and because their sponsorship carries standing exposure, selling is self-vetting: nobody stakes their conduct standing on a bad recruit.

## 1. Target persona — the hubs

Selection fold over the 12 universe ∩ 11 interviewed (G2-known) leads:
```
hub_score(c) = w · [authority_age(c), safety_clean(c), fleet 1–20, corridor_position(c),
               community_centrality(c), interview_signal(c)]
```
- Carriers with aged authority + clean history: credibility incarnate to the new-entrant cohort they'll recruit.
- Yard-owning carriers (the dual-pitch join): they demo yard + dispatch simultaneously.
- Respected dispatchers and driver-trainers: the people new drivers already call for advice.
- Community anchors: freight communities are dense social graphs (diaspora networks, faith communities, truck-stop regulars) — the corpus's constitutional vocabulary is native, not marketing, in several of them; recruit where the trust already lives.

Cohort size per chapter: `n_f ∈ [m_c, ~2·m_c]` — enough to activate, few enough that founding standing means something.

## 2. The offer (founding standing — declared, time-boxed, non-speculative)

| Lever | Form | Constraint honored |
|---|---|---|
| Dues waiver | first k months, declared | fee-for-service intact |
| Sponsorship allotment | elevated invite capacity | exposure scales with capacity — more invites, more standing at risk |
| Founding governance | charter-drafting seats; constants set by those who'll live under them | 1M1V unchanged — no super-votes |
| Own economics | their routed freight through the Face at open-book: the margin they currently surrender returns as patronage from load one | I1/I7; voluntary routing |
| Recognition | founding cohort on-stream, permanent | costless, durable |

Explicitly excluded: share discounts (NAV-only issuance is inviolable — 04 §3), recruitment bonuses (§0), revenue guarantees (risk-shifting).

## 3. The demo kit (every asset fold-backed per 12 P1)

```
onboard(hub) ⇒ demo assets materialize from hub's own exhaust:
  stake-view          — their accruing equity, live
  open-book statement — their loads, the spread they kept
  hours-returned fold — back-office time deltas
  detention/accessorial recoveries — found money on their books
  relay map           — their corridor, home-daily legs rendered
```
The founding member's onboarding *is* the demo-kit build: T0 agents run on their real operation, and the artifacts they show peers are their own numbers. Sandbox mode for prospects: seeded walkthrough on anonymized fixtures (11 §1.5) for demos before commitment. Scripts and one-pagers are versioned assets that fail build on unresolvable claims — the seller cannot oversell; the material won't compile.

## 4. Selling mechanics

- **Peer demo in the yard**: hub shows their phone at the truck stop, the dock, the community hall — the stake-view and open-book screens are the pitch. Native channels ≻ media.
- **Sponsored admission**: prospect accepts → hub sponsors → conduct-standing exposure attaches → onboarding runs A-tier (12 §5), one signature (the prospect's).
- **Shipper-side selling**: hubs' sponsored introductions to their freight relationships seed the Face's credit lines (I7 voluntary; wāsiṭa-pattern provenance) — the same cohort sells both sides of the market.
- **Operator support**: R-queue handles the hub's escalations same-day during campaign; a founding member stuck in a broken flow is negative marketing with standing.

## 5. Anti-pyramid predicate (structural)

```
P: ∄ payment event p: basis(p) ∈ {recruit_count, recruitment_fee, downline_volume}
   sponsorship yields exposure and standing, never income
   founding levers ∈ declared set (§2); off-set inducements unrepresentable
```
This is not compliance hedging; it is the model: a co-op that paid for recruitment would be buying members instead of earning them, importing exactly the adverse selection the conduct gate exists to exclude.

## 6. Campaign operations

- Concentration: corridor-pair geography per 12 §2 — hubs recruited in matched pairs of cities so first corridors activate whole.
- Sequencing: D-track live on the first hub's books *before* the cohort invitation wave (a demo that doesn't run isn't a demo); L/Y funnels open concurrently since their conversion gates sit downstream.
- Cadence: harness interview → founding invitation (the interview close *is* the invitation for hub-scored leads) → onboarding/demo-kit materialization → sponsorship activation.
- Budget: interview honoraria + paid channels per 12 §4; measured on cost-per-activated-hub and second-order admissions per hub — the campaign's real output is sponsors, not signups.

## 7. Properties

```
P1  comp basis excludes recruitment terms (anti-pyramid, unrepresentable)
P2  ∀ demo asset: compiles from folds ∨ declared terms; personal assets from the hub's own stream
P3  founding levers ∈ declared, time-boxed set; share issuance at NAV only
P4  ∀ admission: sponsorship exposure attached; sponsor standing adjusts on sponsored conduct
P5  hub_score, cohort selection, campaign metrics are pure functions of the log
P6  campaign claims inherit 12 P1–P3 (honesty, opt-out, compliance envelopes)
```
Adversarial: inducement side-deals (void — on-stream only), sponsorship farming via straw admissions (corroboration threshold + exposure makes it self-punishing), demo-asset cherry-picking (assets compile from full folds, not curated subsets — selective compilation unrepresentable), founding-standing resale (non-transferable by type).
