# Outreach master index — the whole document set, tied together

*58 documents, one map, two views: **the persona journey** (any person, first page
to last letter) and **the lifecycle arc** (any moment, which papers are live and
which are still gated). The set's one sentence: every relationship this network can
form has its invitation, its evidence, its ceremonies, its reckonings, and its
exit — each written before it was needed, each gated on the thing that makes it
honest. This index adds no rules; **where it and any document disagree, the
document wins** (single authority: the paper owns its paper).*

## The doctrines (held by every document; stated here as a checklist)

1. **Zero numbers** — no claim that doesn't resolve to a fold or a declared term.
2. **Preconditions on the page** — every gated document names its own gates and
   is a draft until they're true.
3. **Misses named first, ours first** — every reckoning opens the ledger against
   ourselves.
4. **Refusal as feature** — what the system won't do, said proudly and early.
5. **No re-pitch, no winback, no counter-offer** after a no or a lifecycle event.
6. **Sovereignty of what was never ours** — edges, phones, employment, genesis;
   named at entrance and exit, honored operationally between.
7. **Unbundling** — membership ⊥ listing ⊥ shares ⊥ tenancy ⊥ instrument ⊥
   employment; every page keeps its separations.
8. **Sender sovereignty** — service co-op, land co-op, chapters speak for
   themselves; templates are gifts, not authority.
9. **Counsel wrappers** — [SECURITIES]/[LEGAL] items carry their tier, and the
   template is a starting point for review, never a bypass.
10. **Privacy floor** — raw notes and trackers live outside the repo; seeds are
    never persisted, echoed, or sent; the k-gate travels with data.
11. **Provenance footers** — every document maps its claims to corpus sections
    (and tests, where the claim is mechanical); an unmappable claim gets cut.
12. **Register per persona** — recognition for the hub, arithmetic for the
    skeptic, grace for the exit; warmth varies, rights never do.

## Order of operations (any new contact, any moment)

1. Find the persona's row; confirm the leftmost ungated document's preconditions
   against the log (and counsel's rules, where wrapped).
2. Send/hand only that document, through its declared channel, per its manners.
3. Record what its internal notes require; keep private material out of the repo.
4. When the next lifecycle event lands, move one cell right — never two.
5. At any exit signal: `offboarding_index.md`, starting with its scope checks.
