# Yard-owner offboarding follow-up — template (when a lot delists)

*The delist every yard document promised: one message, gracious, record handed over.
Three cause variants with different registers — **hers** (zero probing), **the
corridor's** (deactivation explained as design), and **ours** (the lease exit
discipline firing: owned as OUR demand shortfall on her ground, never a judgment of
her lot). One silence absolute: **no contribution-for-shares mention** — the
anti-bait restraint holds hardest at the exit. Trailers off before the letter sends;
series rules throughout.*

---

**Subject:** Closing the [lot] listing — your record, the final accounting, and a clean gate
*(alt: "The delisting, done the way we promised: one message deserved this reply")*

Hi **[first name]**,

**[The cause, plainly — pick one:]**
**[Her choice: "Your delisting message did exactly what we said it would — this
letter is just the paperwork keeping up. No questions about the why; the one-message
exit was the deal, and here it is working."]**
**[Corridor deactivation: "The [corridor]'s staging demand wound down — the same
evidence that brought trailers to your gate stopped bringing them, and the gates
closed the corridor the way they opened it: automatically. Nothing about your lot
changed; the freight did."]**
**[Our non-renewal: "We're not renewing the lease term, and you're owed the real
reason: the utilization our own rules require to keep a lease ran under its
economics — that's OUR demand shortfall on your ground, the exit discipline we
committed to at signing doing its job. It is not a judgment of your lot, which did
everything a lot can do; the freight we promised ourselves we'd only sign against
didn't sustain, and our rules don't let us hold ground on hope."]**

**Trailers:** **[the true state — "the last trailer hooked out [date]; your ground is
clear" — a goodbye letter with trailers still sitting doesn't send; see notes.]**

**The final accounting:** **[per-drop payments — "all [N] drops settled at your card;
nothing open between us." Open items block the letter.]**

**Your record — the protection that outlives the listing:** attached is the complete
custody history of your lot: **every drop and every hook that ever touched your
ground, each with its signed condition report from the moment it happened.** If a
damage question ever surfaces later about a trailer that sat here, the reports are
in your hand, not our archive — the dispute protection you listed for doesn't end
because the listing did.

**What ends, what doesn't:** your listing and its availability come down today; your
declared terms were always yours and go with you. The no-commitments deal ends the
way it ran — **nothing owed, either direction, no wind-down fees, no notice
penalties**; the symmetry that never promised you minimums never asked you for any.
The operational history your lot generated remains what it always was: sealed,
factual, never anyone's marketing.

**The gate back:** relisting is one message, same as leaving —
**[where time will have passed: "plus the twenty-minute walk again if the security
attestation has gone stale; the member-walked gate applies on the way back in the
same way it did the first time, which is exactly why loaded freight trusted your
fences."]**

**What didn't go right across the listing, from us first:**

- **[The honest aggregate — the late hooks, the driver who ignored gate hours and
  his consequence, the payment that slipped and was announced. If the non-renewal
  variant: the demand shortfall itself leads this list, in numbers she can see.]**

Thanks for the asphalt, **[first name]** — it did its job every day the freight
showed up, and that second part was always ours to answer for.

**[FOUNDER — name]**

## Usage notes (internal — do not send)

- **Trailers off BEFORE the letter** — confirmed clear, last hook dated. A goodbye
  over occupied ground is a negotiation, not a goodbye.
- **Settle-before-send** — the series rule; per-drop accounting closed to zero.
- **The record export prepared and checked**: her lot's complete drop/hook/condition
  history, hers to keep — this is the damage-dispute protection made permanent, and
  it's the letter's spine.
- **The non-renewal variant is the hard one — own it fully**: the exit discipline
  (the coverage-ratio signal at work) is OUR rule about OUR demand; "your lot did
  everything a lot can do" must be true and said. Never soften into vague
  "circumstances"; never imply her ground failed. The demand numbers lead her
  misses list because she's owed them.
- **THE CONTRIBUTION SILENCE IS ABSOLUTE**: no shares mention, no "before you go,"
  nothing — at exit the restraint that protected the pitch protects the goodbye.
  If SHE raises it: the reading-session process, at her initiation, later — never
  in the offboarding conversation.
- **Grade-lapse variant** (delisting because the attestation staled and she passed
  on the re-walk): factual, no fault — the gate's freshness requirement is Rosa's
  protection, and letting it lapse is a legitimate choice, not a failure.
- **Relist mechanics accurate**: one message + fresh G2 walk where stale — never
  promise a frictionless return the gate would contradict.
- **Non-member handling**: if she never became a member, nothing about her was ever
  on the log beyond the listing's own records — say nothing about data because
  there's nothing to say, and the silence is the feature.
- **The diner rule, yard edition** — yard owners talk at the county line; the
  non-renewal owned honestly is worth ten pitches.
