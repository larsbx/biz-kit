# The second-chapter chartering kit — for when a new locality forms

*Corpus 12 §6: "chapters instantiate where density crosses m_c: chapter-bootstrap kit =
charter templates + constant defaults + envelope defaults from harness findings.
Chapters are born pre-vetted." This kit is that sentence, unpacked — plus the honest
machinery notes. The deep design to hold onto throughout: **sovereignty is cryptographic
from minute one.** The new chapter is not a branch office; it is a peer that was never,
at any moment, under the first chapter's keys.*

---

## 1 — Sovereignty from birth (the part that must not be improvised)

```
[ ] the new chapter picks its chapter_id: ____________ (an id is all a chapter IS to
    the substrate — the 1D federation test proved a second one joins with ZERO schema
    change; that test was written for this day)
[ ] THEIR key ceremony, THEIR hands: governance/steward/checkpoint keypairs generated
    by the new cohort, TOFU genesis in THEIR chapter_id, multi-holder from day one
    (they get to skip our bootstrap's single-founder phase — learn from us)
[ ] their genesis checkpoint published off-machine BY THEM, held by them
[ ] the first chapter holds NONE of their keys, ever — write it down, mean it
    (00 Art. II sovereignty + IV.5 federation-as-union, made cryptographic)
```

## 2 — What transfers, what's offered, what never moves

| | how |
|---|---|
| **transfers (normative)** | the corpus (their constitution too — they ratify it, session one), the code (one repo, chapter-scoped throughout) |
| **offered as defaults, theirs to change** | the first chapter's declared constants (visible on the log — instructive, never binding) · the adopted D spec + envelope defaults, **ratified by hash**: their governance signs `SpecAdopted` citing the SAME content-addressed artifacts — sovereignty kept, discovery not re-run (the harness findings were the network's, 12 §6) · every session agenda, ceremony script, and checklist in this kit's directory |
| **never moves** | members (membership is per chapter; a person in both = two records, two accounts — 1B design) · funds (nothing to move; nothing ever moves) · standings (conduct standing is earned per chapter; sponsorship bridges, provenance intact) |

## 6 — The federation moment (say it out loud at the joint session)

Two chapters make the federation real — it was a union of one until now, which is to
say implicit. What the federation IS stays exactly what the corpus says: standards, the
protocol, the ruling interface — **never** an operator of chapters (00 Art. IV.5).
Federation aggregates already work (the seam sums sovereign folds — built and tested in
1D for this day); federation authority beyond that accrues only by explicit, signed,
bilateral consent, agreement by agreement. The first chapter's seniority is worth
exactly one vote per member, like everything else.
