# Carrier offboarding follow-up — template (when a member departs)

*The operational unwind letter — sent alongside (never merged with) the member
redemption follow-up: that letter owns his money; this one owns everything else. Its
center is a promise as old as the substrate: **the member export** (SUBSTRATE §7 —
"their own data, portable") finally kept: his complete archive, verifiable offline
forever. Applies on departure or retirement; **the bad-standing variant changes the
warmth, never the rights** — his data is his regardless of why he left. Series rules;
no winback.*

---

**Subject:** Your departure, done properly — the export, the unwind, and what stays yours
*(alt: "Closing your operational file — everything portable, everything listed")*

Dear **[first name]**,

With your departure recorded, this letter does the operational side properly — your
capital account is handled in its own letter, on its own schedule; nothing here
touches money.

**Your export — the promise from before you joined:** attached/delivered is your
complete archive: your registration and key history, your memberships, every
patronage event, your redemption records, and the rule declarations needed to
**reproduce your own balances from scratch** — plus every custody, tender, and
invoice record your operation created. **It verifies offline, forever, with nothing
from us.** This was answered in the substrate's design documents before the first
member signed anything; today it's kept.

**The operational unwind, item by item:**

- **Your envelope is revoked as of your departure** — atomically, on the record: no
  tender will be accepted in your name again, and the revocation's timestamp is
  yours to point at.
- **In-flight loads completed under the terms they started with** — **[the list, or
  "none were open."]**
- **Open obligations on the rail — [list or "none"] — remain exactly as signed.**
  Leaving the co-op doesn't leave your debts or your credits: your key is still
  yours, settlement stays external as always, and discharge attestations work after
  departure the same as before. The record never needed your membership; it needed
  your signature, which you keep.
- **Your streams are sealed, not shredded**: the transaction records your operation
  created are permanent, bilateral, and closed — visible to you and the parties of
  each event, never pooled, never anyone's market intelligence, exactly as during
  membership. Your customers and lanes were never ours to keep; the records of
  loads run are history's, on the same terms as everyone's.

**Your drivers, said clearly:** any of your drivers who are members in their own
right **remain members in their own right** — your departure is yours, not theirs,
in both directions: none of them owes the co-op an explanation for staying, and none
owes you one either. Sovereignty was always per-person here.

**What survives your departure:** your standing (you leave in good standing, on the
record) **[bad-standing variant: state the standing factually, without editorial —
the archive and every right above are identical]** · your sponsorship provenance
(the members you brought in keep your name in their story; your exposure ended with
your membership) · **[founding: "and your founding standing, which no lifecycle
event touches — see your capital letter for the longer version of that sentence."]**

**The door:** re-joining is a fresh membership through the ordinary gate — sponsor,
standing, verification — and your history is on the record helping you, not a
complication. No one will call you about it; the door doesn't ring people.

**What didn't go right across your membership, from us first:**

- **[The honest aggregate — the misses from his trial letters that stayed fixed or
  didn't, the escalations, the friction. A membership's worth of record; name what
  it shows. If he's departing over a grievance: it appears HERE, in his terms,
  with our answer — never omitted because it's awkward.]**

Thank you for hauling with your own name on the door of the whole thing,
**[first name]**.

**[FREIGHT CO-OP GOVERNANCE — names]**
**[contact]**

## Usage notes (internal — do not send)

- **The export is the letter's gate**: prepared from his streams (the 1B-shaped
  member export: members/, memberships/, patronage/, redemptions/, + accrual_rules
  for balance reproduction, + his operational streams), **self-verified before
  delivery**. The §7 promise was made before the first member existed; keeping it
  sloppily is breaking it.
- **Envelope revocation confirmed on the log before drafting** — atomic, timestamped;
  the letter points at it.
- **Open rail obligations LISTED, never summarized** — each with its state; his
  post-departure discharge capability stated because it's true (member keys persist;
  the gate checks keys, not membership, for discharge).
- **The drivers' paragraph is mandatory when he employed driver-members** — the
  moment a fleet departs is exactly when its drivers wonder if they're implicated;
  the sovereignty sentence answers before they ask.
- **Bad-standing departures**: the variant changes tone, never substance — archive,
  obligations, drivers, door (through the ordinary gate, where his standing speaks
  for itself). A censured member's data rights are identical; the letter proves the
  system's fairness precisely when it's hardest.
- **The grievance rule**: if he's leaving angry, his grievance appears in the misses
  section in his terms with our answer — a departure letter that omits the reason
  for the departure is a document about a different member.
- **Two letters, never merged**: this and the redemption letter are separate
  instruments (operations vs capital) on separate schedules — same discipline as
  property-vs-member letters.
- **No winback machinery**: contact expectations cleared; the door line is the last
  word on returning.
