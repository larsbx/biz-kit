# Property-owner redemption follow-up — template (when the first cycle runs)

*The series' final letter, and its most gated: sent only after the land co-op is live
([SECURITIES] preconditions from the one-pager, all of them) AND the first redemption
cycle has completed. **Counsel reviews this letter each cycle before it goes anywhere**
— it is holder communication under the exemption; the bracket is load-bearing exactly
as in the scheduling email. Its audience is the structural insight: **ALL holders
receive it, not just redeemers** — the exit performing honestly is what every remaining
holder's trust rests on, and this letter is the risk paragraph reporting back as fact.
One absolute rule beyond the usual: **no re-buy language, ever** — a redemption report
that pitches is a redemption report that lies about its purpose.*

---

**Subject:** The first redemption cycle — the queue, the gates, and the arithmetic
*(alt: "The exit, measured: what the first cycle actually did")*

Dear **[first name]**,

The land co-op's first redemption cycle has completed. When you bought in, you read a
risk paragraph that said redemptions follow declared gates and may queue, and that the
exit was a process, not a promise of speed. This letter is that paragraph reporting
back with numbers — because a legible exit was the deal, and legible means you see how
it actually behaved, whether or not you used it.

**The cycle's arithmetic:** [N] redemption requests · the period's liquidity gate
allowed [M] · **[M] were paid in full, at the appraised value per share, first-in
first-out — and the order is provable from the record**, which any member can verify.
**[Where a queue formed: "[N−M] requests carried forward in their original order;
nobody moved up, nobody moved back, and the record shows it — including for the
people who could have asked."]**

**Exit without forfeiture, kept:** every redeemer received the same appraised value a
buyer would have paid that period — no haircut, no leaving-penalty, no discount for
the sin of wanting out. That was Article-level (the charter's, and before it the
constitution's), and now it's also precedent.

**The value, honestly:** the appraisal this cycle priced shares at **[the number —
members see their co-op's NAV; COUNSEL confirms presentation]**, which is
**[higher/lower/unchanged]** against last period. **[Where lower: "the risk paragraph
said value can fall; this period it did, and redeemers exited at the fallen value —
that's the no-guarantee being real in both directions. The appraisal basis and its
provenance are attached; dispute paths per the declared protocol."]**

**What the gate held back, and why that's the structure working:** **[where demand
exceeded the gate: "the gate that queued [n] requests is the same gate that kept the
co-op from selling ground at fire-sale speed — ground the rest of you still own. A
fund that pays exits instantly by wrecking remaining holders isn't more liquid; it's
just dishonest one layer later. Ours queues, in the open, in order."]**

**What didn't go right, from us first:**

- **[The honest list — the appraisal that took longer than the protocol's clock, the
  notification that lagged, the process friction a first cycle finds. If nothing:
  the series' standing luck-not-design humility, which after ONE cycle is the only
  honest posture.]**

**[HOLDER-TENANT PARAGRAPH — include for members who also lease space the land co-op
holds (a shop with a bay, a carrier with yard slots): "One thing your redemption does
NOT touch: your lease. Shares and tenancy are separate relationships by design — your

**For those who redeemed in full and are leaving:** thank you, plainly. Your exit at
full value, in order, without friction beyond the declared gates, is the best
advertisement this structure will ever have — and your membership door stays open,
unconnected to any share question, if you ever want back.

**No ask.** This letter reports; it does not invite. The share process remains what
it was — counsel-cleared, membership-first, at your initiation only — and if this
report changes anyone's thinking in either direction, the right response is the same
slow reading session as the first time.

## Usage notes (internal — do not send)

- **Counsel reviews EVERY cycle's letter** — holder communication under the
  exemption; the template is a starting point for that review, never a bypass of it.
  Distribution rules and delivery records same as all share materials.
- **The FIFO provability is the letter's spine**: verify the queue order from the
  log before drafting, and offer the verification path to members. "Nobody moved
  up... including for the people who could have asked" must be checked, not
  assumed — a founder who jumped the queue makes this letter a lie and the co-op
  a fraud; check it FIRST.
- **NAV presentation per counsel** — members see their co-op's numbers, but HOW the
  appraisal figure appears in written holder communication is counsel's call each
  cycle. Attach the appraisal provenance either way.
- **The value-fell variant is mandatory when true** and gets no cushioning — "the
  no-guarantee being real in both directions" is the register. A cycle where NAV
  fell and the letter mumbles is the moment the risk paragraph becomes retroactive
  marketing.
- **Gate-held requests get the refusal-as-feature framing** (the series pattern at
  its highest stakes): the fire-sale sentence is the honest defense of queuing —
  use it only if the gates actually bound; never hypothetically.
- **NO RE-BUY LANGUAGE**: no "opportunity," no "current valuation," no "window" —
  nothing that reads as solicitation. The letter reports the past; the future
  belongs to the reading-session process at the member's initiation. This rule has
  no exceptions and survives every good number.
- **The holder-tenant paragraph** covers every persona whose T3 is "facility equity"
  (shops with bays, carriers with yard slots — the 03 matrix): there is ONE
  redemption letter for all of them, because there is one redemption machinery and
  one counsel review per cycle — persona-forked redemption letters would multiply
  the [SECURITIES] surface for zero mechanical difference (content moves, never
  duplicates). Shares ⊥ tenancy is the paragraph's whole claim: verify it remains
  literally true in the lease terms before including it (a lease clause that
  conditions tenancy on shareholding would make the paragraph a lie AND violate
  04 §3's exit-without-forfeiture spirit — flag it to governance, don't paper it).
- **Redeemer grace**: full-exit members get the thank-you verbatim in spirit —
  their clean exit is structural proof; treat it as the asset it is.
- **Misses after one cycle are guaranteed** — a first cycle with a clean report and
  no misses listed means the misses weren't looked for. Look harder.
