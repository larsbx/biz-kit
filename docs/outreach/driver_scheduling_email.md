# Driver scheduling email — template (the relay-legs interview)

*Companion to `driver_onepager.md` (Track L); books the hour it asked for. Two edges
unique to this persona: **never while rolling** — the interview happens parked, on
reset, or at home, full stop (HOS, safety, and respect all point the same way); and
**zero poaching smell** — if he drives for someone today, this is not a job offer and
must never read like one (00 Art. II.1: drivers are sovereign to their carriers; the
interview is about the network's design and his possible membership, not his current
seat). Zero numbers. Recording defaults OFF for OTR (multi-state consent law is murky
for a moving person — counsel's per-state clearances don't travel at 65 mph).*

## Usage notes (internal — do not send)

- **Never while rolling — enforce it yourself.** If he picks up and he's driving, the
  call is thirty seconds: "call me when you're parked." No exceptions, including his.
- **Time zones move with him** — confirm where he'll be, not where he lives.
- **Remote consent ceremony**: generate on the video call, he writes the seed from
  your screen or photographs it — **never SMS or email the seed** (a transient
  read-back or his own photo leaves no copy on anyone's servers; a text does).
  Everything else per the ceremony script.
- **Recording: OFF for OTR, period**, unless counsel has cleared his specific
  situation — the notes-only path is complete and the murk isn't worth the trust.
- **Poaching hygiene**: if his current carrier is a member or a prospect, extra care —
  no crossover pressure exists in either direction (00 Art. II.1 is a wall, not a
  guideline), and if he asks "should I quit and drive relay," the honest answer is
  that relay legs are corridor-dependent, driver seats belong to member carriers, and
  nobody should quit anything for a map that isn't live.
- **The lease-purchase question WILL come up**: the one-pager's six contrasts are the
  answer; the counsel gate ("offered nowhere until lawyers clear it — anyone offering
  you one today isn't us") is repeated verbatim, every time.
- **Ad-sourced contacts** (12 §1 paid channels): compliance envelope discipline
  applies to the CHANNEL (opt-outs honored, quiet hours); this 1:1 email follows a
  response, never initiates cold.
- **Decline handling**: one thank-you, park it, no sequence — a driver's number
  travels by trust out here, and how you handle "no" is the reputation.
