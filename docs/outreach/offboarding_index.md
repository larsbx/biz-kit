# Offboarding index — the exit letters, tied together

*One page for the founder at the moment an exit lands on the desk: which letter
fires on which trigger, which exits are stacks of more than one letter, and the
doctrine every letter shares — stated here ONCE, so each template's own notes carry
only what's distinct. The rule of the family: **everything the network can begin,
it can end properly — settled, witnessed, and owing nothing.***

---

## First: is it actually a departure?

The most common offboarding error is sending a goodbye to a relationship that
didn't end. Check the trigger against the log before opening any template:

- **A driver who stopped taking legs** → no letter. Voluntary, load by load,
  forever; noticing quiet is surveillance with stationery.
- **A driver (or anyone) who changed employers** → no letter. Membership is
  per-person; it was never the fleet's to take along.
- **A carrier whose fleet departs** → carrier letter to the carrier; **nothing**
  to his driver-members (the carrier letter's drivers paragraph already told
  them their membership is their own).
- **A shipper who merely went quiet** → the shipper letter's drift variant exists,
  but the bar is a real wind-down, not a slow month.
- **A shop/yard/dock delisting** → the listing letter — NOT a membership event;
  membership, where held, continues untouched and the letter says so.
- **A member of a dissolving chapter** → no letter from us at all; the chapter
  letter goes governance-to-governance, and individuals arrive (or don't) at the
  ordinary gate on their own initiative.

## The stacks — exits that are more than one letter

Some departures fire several letters, each on its own schedule, **never merged**:

- **Carrier member departs** → capital: `member_redemption_followup.md` ·
  operations: `carrier_offboarding_followup.md`.
- **Worker departs the service co-op** → capital: `mechanic_redemption_followup.md`
  · operations: `mechanic_offboarding_followup.md`.
- **Founding member departs** → capital (incl. the founding section):
  `member_redemption_followup.md` · operations: `carrier_offboarding_followup.md`
  · offices: `hub_offboarding_followup.md` — three files; the standing is in none
  of them because it doesn't leave.
- **Any departing member who also holds land-co-op shares** → the property letters
  apply *in addition*, from the land co-op, per counsel — one machinery each,
  neither absorbs the other.
- **Holder-tenant redeeming to zero** → `property_offboarding_followup.md` with
  the holder-tenant bracket; the lease letter that doesn't exist is the point
  (shares ⊥ tenancy — nothing about the tenancy ends, so nothing about it sends).

## The doctrine — stated once

Every letter in the family obeys all of these; a letter that can't is not ready
to send:

1. **Settle before send.** Money to zero, trailers off, truck back, keys revoked,
   netting attested — the physical and financial unwind *precedes* the letter;
   open items make it a negotiation, not a goodbye.
2. **The record goes with them.** Export / archive / condition history /
   checkpoint witness — complete, self-verified before delivery, verifying
   offline forever. The going-away gift is always the same gift: their own
   history, portable.
3. **Misses named first, ours first.** Every letter carries the honest aggregate
   of what we got wrong across the relationship's life; where the cause is
   disuse or non-renewal, OUR demand shortfall leads the list, in their numbers.
4. **Bad standing changes warmth, never rights.** Export, settlement,
   attestations, doors — identical for the censured; the system proves its
   fairness precisely when it's hardest.
5. **The grievance rule.** Anyone departing over a grievance sees it in the
   letter, in their terms, with our answer — a departure letter that omits the
   departure's reason is about a different member.
6. **No winback. No re-pitch. No counter-offer after the lifecycle event.** The
   door back is stated once, factually, and it does not ring. Near shares this
   hardens to: no re-buy language, ever.
7. **What was never ours doesn't end.** Phone numbers, customer edges, direct
   relationships, their genesis, their story — named as sovereign at the exit
   exactly as at the entrance, and honored operationally (untracked, unfollowed).
8. **Unbundling holds to the last page.** Listing ⊥ membership · shares ⊥ vote ·
   shares ⊥ tenancy · instrument ⊥ membership · employment ⊥ network — each
   letter proves its separation in the direction the exit tests.
9. **Sender sovereignty.** Each relationship's own organ sends its own letter —
   service co-op, land co-op, surviving chapter; the freight co-op never speaks
   for a peer.
10. **Counsel where the wrapper requires** — every property/share instance
    (Tier 3.2), separation paperwork shapes (3.3/3.4), estate carve-outs; the
    template is a starting point for review, never a bypass.
11. **The precedent is aggregate.** Each first exit handled per its letter is
    reported to governance as aggregate fact; individual figures stay bilateral.

## Order of operations (any exit)

1. Confirm the trigger on the log (see the scope checks above).
2. Identify the stack — which letters fire, from which senders, on which
   schedules.
3. Execute the unwind the letter will report: settle, return, revoke, attest.
4. Prepare and self-verify the export/record.
5. Draft from the template; run its internal notes as a checklist; counsel where
   flagged.
6. Send; record delivery where the wrapper requires.
7. Report the aggregate precedent to governance; then honor the silence — the
   file is closed, and closed files don't ring.
