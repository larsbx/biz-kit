# Biz-kit ethos

AAA describes what a business wants for itself. TTT describes what businesses
need from one another and from Biz-kit when they work together.

## AAA — Wants

- **Authority.** Each business decides who can act on its behalf. Every
  commitment can be traced to the authority behind it.
- **Autonomy.** Each business chooses whom to work with, what to commit to, and
  which decisions to delegate to people or software.
- **Access.** Businesses can use shared services on clear terms, and their
  authorized representatives can reach the tools and records they need.

## TTT — Needs

- **Truth.** The record shows what the evidence establishes, which claims are
  still open, and how errors were corrected.
- **Transparency.** Each party can see and understand the terms, costs, and
  decisions that affect it, with confidential information protected.
- **Trust.** Businesses earn trust by keeping commitments, applying agreed
  rules consistently, and resolving disputes.

## Example — Cooperative group purchase

Each member business sees its share of the supplier price and service costs
before choosing whether to participate. An authorized representative approves
the commitment for that business. The business can reconcile the charge against
the supplier invoice and delivery record and use those records to raise a
discrepancy.
