# Biz-Kit commercial clearing

Use only for participating businesses; individuals act as authorized agents of
business principals.

## Prepare a setoff proposal

1. Resolve both business principals and independently verify each agent's authority.
2. Load exact eligible source obligations and their immutable source revisions.
3. Reject duplicates, stale revisions, mixed principals, unsupported currencies, or
   obligations already consumed by irreversible lineage.
4. For same-currency setoff, bind exact source obligations and signed aggregates.
5. For cross-currency analysis, bind the rational rate numerator and denominator,
   rounding rule, exact integer result, residuals, terms hash, and source-event
   commitments. A preview is not authorization.
6. Obtain both businesses' signatures over the same domain-separated commitment.
7. Revalidate authorization and concurrency immediately before admission.
8. Return the admitted event/receipt or a refusal. Never describe an unsigned or
   merely signed proposal as accounting discharge, settlement, payment, or transfer.

## Legal and fiqh boundaries

State the narrow proposition being evaluated. A non-prohibition determination is not
a license, regulatory approval, fatwa, or finding that every implementation is lawful.
Keep contract classification, accounting recognition, commercial discharge, external
settlement, tax, and money-transmission analysis distinct.

## Evidence

Always expose exact sources, commitment digest, signer identities and authority
epochs, currency/rate terms, residuals, event lineage, and verification status.
