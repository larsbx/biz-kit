# Project Instructions — Freight Cooperative Platform

## Mission

A member-owned freight cooperative and its open-source platform delivering **the complete line**: origin dock to destination across activated, participating corridors (leased third-party docks), staged through per-city yards for drop-and-hook on both sides — maximally automated (headless: agents + folds; humans at lax-direction signatures only), middlemen removed with every captured rent resolving to member-visible flows, and member capital accumulation maximized within the deontic limits that are constitutional constraints. Yards are clock decouplers (staging removes the live-load detention category at coverage-cleared endpoints); the yard graph enables relay linehaul (home-daily legs where geometry and freight support them — the capacity moat). Federation of sovereign locality chapters; the app is the coordination layer and the primary membership incentive (ownership). Adjacent verticals: informal-economy labor pooling; licensed trades (Detroit renovation likely first).
