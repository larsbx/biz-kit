# Demo-day checklist — the founding cohort presentation

*The day the invitation letter promised: the cohort in a room, and the proof
running live. Three rules give the day its shape: **the hub presents, not the
founder** — it's his operation, shown by him, on his phone (the one-pager's
promise, kept literally); **every number is a fold over the full stream** — the
founder cannot cherry-pick their own pitch, so the bad weeks are on screen with
the good ones; and **nothing is signed today** — the demo earns the charter
session; it doesn't collect on it. The demo IS the pitch; everything else in the
room is logistics.*

---

## 0 — Preconditions (all true, or the day waits)

- [ ] `dispatch_closeout_checklist.md` closed — acceptance met, suite green,
      checkpoint published
- [ ] The hub's demo consent list in hand, item by item — **what's on screen
      today is exactly that list**; his books made the proof, his yes makes it
      showable
- [ ] The invitation letter went out per its own rules; today's attendees are
      its cohort (plus whoever a founding-candidate brought — trust travels;
      badges don't)
- [ ] Counsel state re-checked: nothing shown or said today that a tier still
      gates (no instrument terms, no share talk — those documents wait where
      they always wait)

## 1 — Room setup (before anyone arrives)

- [ ] The hub's phone mirrors to the screen; the founder's laptop is the BACKUP,
      not the show
- [ ] The live checkpoint verified in the room (`verify_checkpoint` against the
      published head) — the tamper-evidence demonstration is part of the demo,
      not an appendix
- [ ] The recompute is rehearsed once: pick a fold, replay it from the log in
      front of the room, same number — the thirty seconds that separate this
      from every slideshow they've survived
- [ ] Printed at the door: `field_faq.md` · the founding invitation letter (for
      the friend someone brought) · NOTHING with a number on it — numbers live
      in the folds, on screen, recomputable
- [ ] The no-recording courtesy stated as people arrive: the hub's books are on
      screen by his consent, scoped to this room — phones down during the
      open-book section

## 2 — The demo (the hub's hour)

- [ ] The hub opens with his own before/after, in his own words — the founder's
      only job in this section is running the cable
- [ ] The open-book fold: his operation's real numbers — hours returned,
      detention recovered, escalations and how each resolved — **computed live
      over the FULL stream, misses on screen with the wins** (the week the
      import lagged is in the fold; leaving it in is the credibility)
- [ ] The verification moment: an attendee picks the fold, the room watches the
      replay produce the same number — "you don't have to trust him; that's the
      whole point" said out loud
- [ ] The refusals, said proudly (the anti-pitch): recruitment pay
      unrepresentable — verified as an ABSENCE in front of them · money never
      moves on-platform · no interest anywhere by construction · contested
      things enter at not-yet · every act needs a signature it can point to
- [ ] What the demo does NOT show, named so nobody wonders: other members'
      records (bilateral, forever), edges (his customers are his), anything the
      hub's consent list didn't include

## 3 — Questions (the founder's section)

- [ ] `field_faq.md` register: misses named first when a question walks into
      one; "not yet" said plainly where it's the true answer; the wall
      questions (employment, others' data) get the wall
- [ ] The scheme question, welcomed when it comes: the structural answer (the
      absence verified in §2), never indignation — the hub one-pager's
      "prove it isn't, with structure"
- [ ] Any question with a [SECURITIES]/[LEGAL] answer gets the gate, verbatim:
      designed, written, offered nowhere until counsel clears it — "anyone
      offering you one today isn't us"

## 4 — The close (no pens in the room)

- [ ] What's next, stated exactly once: the charter session — its date, what the
      cohort will decide there (dues, thresholds, every constant), one member
      one vote · membership conversations happen individually, at each person's
      pace, through the ordinary gate
- [ ] **Nothing is signed today, and no one is asked to.** The demo's whole
      argument is that this thing doesn't need pressure to be believed — a
      signup sheet in the room would refute the hour that preceded it
- [ ] Each attendee leaves with: the one-pager for their persona (only where
      its preconditions hold) · the founder's contact · nothing to fill out
- [ ] The friend someone brought: same materials, same pace, sponsor noted in
      the tracker later (private dir, as always)

## 5 — After the room empties

- [ ] The hub's debrief, first: what he'd change about showing his own books,
      any question that crossed his consent line (if one did: it's OUR miss —
      the boundary gets restated in the next room, and he hears the apology
      today)
- [ ] Attendance and follow-up state to the tracker — private dir, never the
      repo; interest recorded as each person's stated next step, not our
      hoped-for one
- [ ] Misses-first, demo edition, written same-day: the fold that stumbled, the
      question that had no good answer, the moment the founder talked when the
      hub should have — next demo's opening material
- [ ] Charter session logistics confirmed to the cohort per the agenda's own
      checklist; the wave letters' claims audited once more against what the
      room actually saw — **the letter may promise less than the demo showed,
      never more**
