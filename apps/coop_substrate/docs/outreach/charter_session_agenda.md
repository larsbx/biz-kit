# Charter-drafting session one — agenda

*The founding meeting the invitation letter convenes. Scoping rule (corpus 00 Art.
IV.2): constants are declared **before first evaluation** — so today decides only
what's imminent; everything else is read aloud as deliberately deferred and stays
**closed until declared**. That default is the room's comfort: an undecided question
never becomes an accidental yes. Facilitation: [FOUNDER] chairs and votes exactly once,
like everyone (00 Art. IV.1 applies to this room before it applies to anything else).
Nothing binds until signed on the log at the table — item 6 is the meeting.*

**Pre-reads sent with the invitation:** the constitution (docs/corpus/00, two pages) ·
the founding offer sketch (13 §2 levers) · "how the signing works" (one paragraph:
decisions become signed, public-to-members events; a checkpoint seals the meeting).

---

## 0 — Welcome + how today works *(15 min)*

The demo recap ([HUB], live, two minutes — his phone, his numbers). Then the rules of
the room: one member one vote, skepticism is service, timeboxes are real, and anything
we can't settle today defaults to closed — deciding to defer IS a decision, and a safe
one.

## 1 — The constitution: ratify, don't draft *(30 min)*

Read-through of 00 (sovereignty, the financial articles, 1M1V, protected objectives).
It is the thing everything else already enforces; today's question is only: **does this
cohort adopt it as supreme?** Objections are recorded and become flagged amendment
candidates (signed governance events, later — Art. VII), never hallway edits.
*Decision: adopt / adopt-with-flagged-objections.*

## 2 — Membership terms (the constants people feel first) *(60 min)*

| decision | shape | default if undecided |
|---|---|---|
| dues: amount + cadence | fee-for-service, access + member rate (04 §4) — dues are never a profit line | no dues collected yet |
| founding waiver period | the 13 §2 lever, time-boxed, same for all founders | no waiver — dues start when dues start |
| admission flow | sponsor with standing exposure + corroboration threshold k (04 §2) — pick k | admissions stay closed |
| probation | the lifecycle exists (invited → probationary → member); decide its length/what confirms | steward judgment, flagged interim |

## 3 — Governance operations *(45 min)*

- **The operator role** (04 §7): appointment of [FOUNDER or other] as a *typed
  capability set, not a position of trust*; compensation declared — fee or salary,
  **never a spread** (the rent-resolution rule applied first to ourselves).
- **The key ceremony**: governance keys re-declared as **multiple holders** — the
  founder's bootstrap key was trust-on-first-use, published as such; today the cohort
  succeeds it properly (who holds a governance key: ____, ____, ____). Steward and
  checkpoint keys reviewed. *This is the single most concrete transfer of power on the
  agenda.*
- Meeting cadence + quorum for future governance events.

## 4 — The founding offer, finalized *(30 min)*

Waiver period (from item 2) · invitation allotment per founder (exposure scales with
it — more invites, more standing at risk) · **the founding-window close** (date or
member-count — 13 §8.1, the cohort's call). Read the exclusions aloud one more time so
they're the room's words, not the paper's: no recruitment pay, no share discounts, no
guarantees.

## 5 — The deferred list, read aloud *(15 min)*

Everything in the 04 §9 registry that today does NOT declare — lease gates, insurance,
land co-op, throughput/floor economics, accrual weights — each waking only when its
program does, each entering contested territory at N (09). *No decision; the reading is
the record that deferral was chosen, not forgotten.*

## 6 — The signing *(30 min — this is the meeting)*

Each decision above becomes its event, signed at the table: `CharterConstantDeclared`
per constant · `RoleKeyDeclared` per new key holder (the ceremony) · adoption of 00
recorded. Then one checkpoint, emitted and **published off-machine while everyone
watches**, plus the one-paragraph instruction sheet: how any member verifies, from
their own copy, that tonight happened exactly as they remember it.

## 7 — Legal sync + close *(15 min)*

Counsel status (the charter-on-log and the legal entity's documents must end up saying
the same thing — counsel reviews before any filing; that review is now live, and was
deliberately not requested before this cohort existed). Session two teaser: patronage
and accrual weights, when the first thing bills. Adjourn.
