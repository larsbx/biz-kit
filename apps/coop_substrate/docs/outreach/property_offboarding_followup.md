# Property-owner offboarding follow-up — template (when shares wind down)

*Sent to an individual holder whose redemption completed to ZERO — the cycle report
(the redemption letter) tells all holders how the exit behaved in aggregate; this
letter closes one holding, personally. **The most counsel-gated goodbye in the set:
holder communication under the exemption, counsel reviews every instance before it
goes anywhere** — the template is a starting point for that review, never a bypass.
Sender: the land co-op's own governance. Its work is auditing the one-pager's
promises against HIS record: his queue wait in his numbers, exit-without-forfeiture
as his own arithmetic, and the unbundling proven in the direction nobody tests —
zero shares touches his economics, never his vote, never his lease. One absolute
beyond the family's: **no re-buy language, ever.***

**Subject:** Your redemption, complete — the whole holding, audited

Dear **[first name]**,

Your final redemption has settled, and your share account stands at zero. Before you
bought a single share you read a page that made specific promises about this exact
moment — so this letter is that page, audited against your own record, which is
attached and verifies on its own.

**The exit you actually got:** your last **[n]** shares redeemed at
**[the appraised value per share — COUNSEL confirms presentation]**, the same value
a buyer would have paid that period — **no haircut, no leaving-penalty, no discount
for the sin of wanting out.** Exit without forfeiture was Article-level when you
read it; in your file it's now arithmetic.

**Your wait, measured, because it's yours:** your redemption request(s) queued
**[the honest history — "your final request waited [n] periods behind [m] earlier
requests, and the record proves nobody passed you"]**. The gates that made you wait
are the same gates that kept the co-op from selling ground at fire-sale speed —
ground other members still own — but the waiting was still yours to do, and it
belongs in your file as a number, not a shrug.

**The value story, uncushioned:** across your holding, the appraised value moved
**[the true path — bought at X, redeemed at Y, period by period in the attached
record]**. **[Where he exited below his buy-in: "You exited at less than you paid.
The risk paragraph said value can fall, and for your holding it did — that's the
no-guarantee being real in both directions, and this letter won't dress it as
anything else."]**

**Your record — the holding, portable:** attached is your complete share history:
every buy-in with its appraisal and provenance, every distribution with the
utilization that produced it, every queue position with its first-in first-out
proof, and the final settlement. **It verifies offline, forever** — the appraisals
that priced your ground carry their provenance with them.

**What your zero balance does not touch:**

- **Your membership and your vote.** Membership came first and was never
  conditional on holding — and your vote never scaled with shares while you held
  them, which is exactly why it doesn't shrink now that you don't. One member, one

  every member. Your landlord's books changed; your rent, your term, and your
  standing at the counter did not."]**

**What ends:** your economic participation — distributions and share-value
exposure, both of which followed the shares out the door, as declared.

## Usage notes (internal to the LAND CO-OP's governance — do not send)

- **Counsel reviews EVERY instance** — holder communication under the exemption,
  same discipline as the cycle report; distribution and delivery records same as
  all share materials. Estate- or incapacity-driven wind-downs follow counsel's
  process entirely; this template applies only after counsel adapts it.
- **Settle-before-send, provably**: the final redemption settled on the record,
  the FIFO history verified from the log before drafting — "nobody passed you"
  is checked, not assumed, same as the cycle report's rule.
- **The exited-at-a-loss variant is mandatory when true and gets no cushioning** —
  his personal loss is where the risk paragraph either proves honest or becomes
  retroactive marketing. Period-by-period value path attached either way.
- **His wait named by us first** — the personal edition of the cycle report's
  gate-defense: the fire-sale sentence may explain the wait, never excuse it from
  appearing in his numbers.
- **The unbundling verified, not recited**: vote unchanged on the log; where the
  holder-tenant bracket applies, re-verify the lease carries no shareholding
  condition (the redemption letter's rule — a conditioned lease is flagged to
  governance, never papered).
- **NO RE-BUY LANGUAGE** — no "opportunity," no "current valuation," no "window."
  The way-back paragraph states the door and the silence; that's its entire job.
- **The export self-verified before delivery** — buy-ins, appraisals with
  provenance, distributions, queue proofs, settlement.
- **His exit as aggregate precedent**: the cycle report carries it to remaining
  holders; nothing about his individual file goes anywhere else.
- **Misses-first / bad-standing / grievance / no-winback** — the family's rules,
  unchanged by the [SECURITIES] wrapper.
