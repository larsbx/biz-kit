# Carrier trial follow-up — template (after the first Face loads)

*The member-side twin of `shipper_trial_followup.md`, sent after a member carrier's
first loads hauled for the Face. Structural difference: he's an OWNER — his receipts
aren't attached, they're live on his own open-book view; this email points, it doesn't
enclose. Two conversations unique to this side: **payment honesty per load** (the
pay-when-paid posture from onboarding, now kept in detail), and **the envelope
review** — his escalation data offered as observation, never pressure; widening is
his, and all-R remains legitimate forever. Same doctrine as the shipper letter:
misses named by us first; a bad-trial variant that never spins.*

---

**Subject:** Your first [N] Face loads — the open book, kept open
*(alt: "The spread, itemized — and two things that didn't go right")*

Hi **[first name]**,

Your first [N] loads through the desk are done, and everything below is already on
your open-book view — this email is just the walk-through, and twenty minutes by phone
if you want to go line by line.

**The margin, itemized.** Every load shows the shipper rate, your rate, and the spread
between — the number brokers built a business on you not seeing. Your patronage share
of it is accruing under the weights the charter set; the accrual view shows each
credit and the rule that made it. **[If founding cohort: "you voted on those weights —
here they are, working."]**

**Payment, honestly, per load:** **[the true list — "loads 1, 2, 4: shipper paid, your
payment cleared. Load 3: the shipper hasn't paid; per the terms we set at onboarding,
neither have we — the dunning ladder is at rung [x] and you can watch it move. That's
pay-when-paid being what we said it was, including the part where it's slower than
either of us likes."]** Faster-pay stays evidence-gated; the gate's status is visible,
not vague.

**Your envelope, reviewed — your call, our data:** of the [N+M] tenders that came your
way, **[X] auto-accepted inside your envelope, [Y] came to you as questions, [Z]
declined.** One observation, offered and then dropped: **[where true: "you said yes to
all [Y] escalations — that usually means the boundary sits tighter than your actual
comfort, and widening it would save you [Y] interruptions. Or it means the questions
are exactly where you want them. Both are right answers; the envelope is yours."]**
Adjusting it is one signed revision, any day.

**What didn't go right, from us first:**

- **[The honest list — the tender that took too long to quote, the document mixup,
  the exception and how it surfaced. If nothing: the same luck-not-design humility
  as the shipper letter.]**

**No ask, except the standing one**: keep routing what works, load by load, skip what
doesn't — the desk keeps earning it or it stops getting it. And one door that's yours
alone: if a shipper of yours should ever meet this desk, your introduction — signed,
bounded, yours — is how they'd get the same receipts you're seeing. Mentioned once,
never pushed.

Thanks for going first on this too, **[first name]** —

**[FACE OPERATOR — name]**
**[phone]**

## Usage notes (internal — do not send)

- **Verify his open-book view renders correctly BEFORE sending** — the member-side
  equivalent of self-verifying the shipper's exports. An owner whose view is broken
  learns the book isn't really open.
- **Payment status accurate to the minute of sending**, and slow payment stated in
  the letter, never buried — the onboarding email's pay-when-paid honesty is either
  kept here in detail or it was marketing.
- **The envelope observation is data-then-silence**: state the escalation pattern,
  offer both readings, close with "the envelope is yours," and never raise it again
  unless he does. Chronic-approval-means-tight-bounds is the cockpit's own logic
  offered at member scale — as a mirror, not a nudge. All-R by choice stays a fully
  respected configuration.
- **Patronage: show the weights, don't paraphrase them** — the accrual view with
  rule ids is the answer to "how was this computed," per the session-two discipline.
- **The sponsor-introduction line appears ONCE** across the whole relationship —
  it's in this letter, so it's used up. His edges are sovereign; a second ask would
  be pressure on exactly the data the constitution protects.
- **Misses first, same doctrine as the shipper letter** — an owner who finds the miss
  before you name it learns the open book has closed pages.
- **BAD-TRIAL VARIANT**: if the desk served him badly — name it, state the structural
  change (which gate, which process, which envelope default was wrong in the adopted
  spec and what the revision path is), and accept that a founding member's patience
  is standing you're spending. Never the good-letter with soft edges.
- **No volume anything, either direction** — same as everywhere.
