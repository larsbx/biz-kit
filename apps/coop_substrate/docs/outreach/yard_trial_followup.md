# Yard-owner trial follow-up — template (after the first staged trailers)

*Follows the walkthrough and listing; sent after the first real trailers staged on her
lot. The review her pitch earned: the two anxieties (damage, liability) get their
RESULTS, not reassurances; payment status is per-drop honest; utilization is reported
plainly because it was never promised. **One line is deliberately absent: the
contribution-for-shares conversation** — disclosed once at pitch to prevent
bait-and-switch, and raising it at first follow-up would look like the bait arriving.
It waits for the seasons the one-pager said. Zero forward promises, as ever.*

---

**Subject:** Your first [N] trailers — the record, drop by drop

Hi **[first name]**,

The first trailers have come and gone from your lot — here's the full account, and
twenty minutes in person if you'd rather walk it than read it.

**What staged:** [N] drops, [N] hooks, at the terms you declared. **Every single one
carried its signed condition report at the moment it happened** — the record is
attached/printable, and it's yours to keep regardless of anything.
**[Where true: "Nothing was disputed."]** **[Where a dispute happened: "The one damage
question on [date] reduced to exactly what we said it would — comparing the drop
report against the hook report — and closed in [timeframe]. That's the machinery you
were skeptical about, doing its one job."]**

**Payment, per drop:** **[the true list — "all [N] drops paid at your card, settled

that date slips, you'll hear it from me before you notice it."]** Your rate schedule
stays yours — revisable forward any time, never renegotiated truck by truck.

**The coverage gate, in practice:** **[where it fired: "[n] loaded trailers were
HELD BACK from your lot because a coverage check wasn't fresh — you never saw them,

make exceptions."]** **[Where it never fired: "every loaded trailer that staged had
passed the check — the gate was quiet because nothing tried to get past it."]**

**Utilization, plainly:** [N] drops against the capacity you listed. **[Honest
gloss — "busier than the corridor's first month suggested" / "thinner than either of
us would like — staging follows the corridor's freight, we promised no minimums in

**What didn't go right, from us first:**

**No ask, except the standing one:** keep the listing if it's earning its place on

Thanks for letting the first trailers prove it on your ground, **[first name]** —

## Usage notes (internal — do not send)

- **The condition-report record prepared and checked before sending** — it's the
  letter's spine and her pitch's promise; a gap in it is the first thing to fix and
  the first thing to name.
- **Payment status accurate to the day**: settlement is external and attested (money
  never moves on-platform) — the letter states what's paid, what's due, and any slip
  BEFORE she notices it. The yard's trust dies at the first surprise invoice-chase;
  this letter exists to make chasing unnecessary.
- **The coverage-gate paragraph**: held-back trailers are the gate WORKING — frame
  them as protection delivered, never as lost revenue to apologize for.
- **Utilization: report, never promise** — thin months said plainly with the
  corridor-dependence restated; the no-minimums symmetry from the pitch is the
  frame. Never project next month.
- **Misconduct transparency**: if a member's driver broke her lot's rules, she learns
  what happened AND that it had consequences (standing review) — her rules being
  enforceable on our side is half the relationship. Don't name the driver beyond
  what's needed; do name the consequence.
- **THE CONTRIBUTION RESTRAINT**: do not mention contribution-for-shares. It was
  disclosed once, at pitch, precisely so it never feels like a trap — the earliest
  it's raised again is after a genuinely strong utilization HISTORY (seasons, the
  one-pager's word), and only if [SECURITIES] counsel has cleared that conversation's
  form by then. First-follow-up mention = the bait arriving.
- **Her lot's dwell/usage numbers are hers to see** — bilateral, shown on request,
  same symmetry as every measured party.
- **Delist handling**: gracious, instant, record handed over — a lot that leaves
  well speaks well at the diner where yard owners actually talk.
