## What this is

A brokerage owned by its carriers — a cooperative's freight desk, running the
[corridor] lane(s) your freight already moves. Not a load board, not a venture-funded
margin machine: the carriers who haul for it own it, and here's why that matters to
*you* — **the margin on every load is disclosed to those carrier-owners.** You're
dealing with a broker whose principals watch its books from the inside, which kills
the games brokers play with both sides of a load. We can't hide a spread from our
owners, so we've stopped trying to hide anything from anyone.

## What working with it looks like *(each of these is the system, not a service tier)*

- **One credit application, signed once.** After that, tenders inside your line move
  without ceremony — quotes back in moments where the lane is live, because the
  pricing runs inside pre-declared corridors, not somebody's mood.
- **A position feed instead of check-call voicemails.** Status comes from telematics
  events, not from someone phoning your carrier to ask.
- **A signed custody record for every participating touch of your freight** — dock to
  dock where the full line runs through network stages — and it's **exportable and
  independently verifiable**: your auditors can check it with no access to our
  systems at all. That's not a feature we turned on for you; it's the only way this
  network knows how to move anything.
- **Accessorial line items arrive with their evidence attached** — arrive/depart
  signatures against the appointment — **or they cannot be invoiced at all.** The
  quarterly invoice war becomes a document comparison, and the documents were signed
  at the moment, not reconstructed for the argument.
- **Your receiving docks are measured the same way everyone is** — from signed events,
  floored, and **you see the same measurements we do.** A site of yours that improves
  its turn times sees it where it counts. Nobody's grading you on anecdotes, in
  either direction.

## What we will never do

Your lanes, volumes, and rates are **bilateral** — visible to you and to the party
you're transacting with, never published, never pooled into anyone's market
intelligence; the carrier who introduced us cannot expose you beyond the introduction
he signed, and neither can we · no rate or savings promises on paper — where the lane
is live, we quote it live, and the quote is the claim · no lock-in: every load is its
own decision, and the exit is the same as never having entered.

## The ask

One lane, a handful of loads, your hardest one if you like — judged on the receipts.
The credit application takes [CARRIER]'s introduction and one signature; after that
the freight decides.
