# Carrier go-live checklist — the first accepted tender

*The first load's third vantage point: the Face checklist owns the network's
gates, the shipper's owns her dock — this one owns HIS operation's night: the
first tender through the Face that his own signed envelope admits. Its exam is
the envelope itself: **the first tender that reaches him proves what can never
reach him** — his lanes, his floors, his ceiling, doing their first live
filtering, in his favor. Its hardest refusals are the two pressures night one
invents: the envelope-expansion ask ("could you raise the ceiling just for this
one") and bypass guilt (his own freight routed around the Face tonight is FINE —
the ad said load by load, and night one is when that's cheap to forget).
Inherited rules: real freight, misses-first tonight, the trial letter owns the
weeks.*

---

## 0 — Preconditions (his terms, live)

- [ ] **His envelope SIGNED on the log, in his words** — lanes, floors,
      ceiling, policy — read back to him before any tender flows; the
      envelope is his instrument, and night one runs on what HE wrote, not on
      what onboarding remembers
- [ ] `tenderable(c)` true through the coverage predicates — authority,
      insurance, the declared chain — verified fresh, never assumed from the
      onboarding file
- [ ] The margin-visibility working FOR HIM: as a member-owner he sees the
      spread on any load he routes — demo it on the test log before his first
      real one carries money
- [ ] His dispatch/paperwork arrangement confirmed as HE runs it — whether
      he's on the tool or on his own back office, the tender rail meets his
      operation where it is
- [ ] His stake machinery ready if member: the patronage rule that will
      credit tonight declared, with its rule id — nothing accrues that can't
      show its rule

## 1 — The tender (the envelope's exam)

- [ ] Real freight, quoted at the declared corridor pricing — the same
      no-loss-leader rule as the shipper's side; a rate bent to win his first
      yes poisons the second exactly as it does hers
- [ ] **The tender fits his envelope, and he's shown the fit**: which lane
      term admitted it, which floor it clears, where his ceiling sits — the
      envelope's first live decision made visible, because "nothing routes
      outside your policy" is tonight's checkable claim, not a brochure line
- [ ] His acceptance signed, explicit, his — and his decline costless, said
      in words: the next tender that fits arrives the same way regardless
- [ ] **Bypass blessed out loud**: if his own customer's freight moves tonight
      too, outside the Face, nothing about that is noticed, counted, or
      commented on — his book is his; the tender rail is an offer, not an
      audit

## 2 — The load (his operation, our silence)

- [ ] His fleet runs it his way — which driver, which truck, his call
      entirely (the employment wall from the network's side: we tender to his
      authority, never into his roster); if his driver is a member running a
      first solo, the driver checklist runs separately, its own night
- [ ] Custody per the one-pattern at every touch — his operation's first
      network chain complete and verifiable
- [ ] Nothing while his truck rolls — the standing absolute applies to his
      drivers exactly as to member drivers

## 3 — The money (the owner's view, working)

- [ ] The invoice evidence-bound — detention and accessorials with their
      hashes or absent, in BOTH directions (what he bills the network's
      shipper and what anyone bills him)
- [ ] **The margin disclosed to him same-day** — the spread on his own load,
      in his own view, as promised to him as an owner from the first
      conversation; load one, not "once the dashboard is ready" (the Face
      checklist's rule, landing on his screen)
- [ ] Settlement external, per the declared terms, on time — the slip rule
      (announced before noticed) live from settlement one
- [ ] His patronage credited with the rule id visible **[non-member: nothing
      accrues, nothing implied — the membership conversation keeps its own
      pace]**

## 4 — Same-day after

- [ ] His debrief at his cadence: did the tender match the envelope's
      promise, did the paper match the freight, did the money match the
      disclosure — his answers lead the misses list
- [ ] Misses-first, tender-one edition, written tonight: the tender detail
      that arrived wrong, the event that posted slow, the disclosure that
      lagged an hour — ours first, in his numbers
- [ ] The weekly status carrier block: tenders offered ____, in-envelope
      ____/____ (this ratio is 100% forever or the gate broke), accepted 1,
      chains complete 1/1, margin disclosed same-day 1/1
- [ ] The carrier trial letter's clock starts (`carrier_face_followup.md`
      owns the weeks); escalations to the R-queue, assigned not admired

## 5 — What does NOT happen tonight (refusals, verified)

- [ ] **No envelope-expansion pressure** — no "raise the ceiling just this
      once," no "add the lane while we're here"; his envelope changes when HE
      re-signs it, at his initiative, cold — never mid-tender, never warm
- [ ] **No bypass penalty, no bypass bookkeeping** — freight he routes around
      the Face is invisible to us by design; the count of his bypasses does
      not exist, and its nonexistence is checkable
- [ ] No tender outside the envelope "for him to just look at" — what his
      policy excludes doesn't reach him even as a teaser; the filter is the
      product
- [ ] No reaching into his roster — which driver runs it is never our
      suggestion
- [ ] No mining his book: his customers, his lanes outside this tender, his
      rates elsewhere — not collected, not inferred, not "insighted"
- [ ] No volume talk in either direction — one accepted tender predicts
      nothing and obligates nothing, and the trial letter will say the same
- [ ] No announcement beyond the folds: one tender, in-envelope, one chain,
      one disclosed margin — counts, not adjectives
