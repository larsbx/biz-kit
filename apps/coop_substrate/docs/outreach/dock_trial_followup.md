# Dock-manager trial follow-up — template (after the first window bookings)

*Follows the walkthrough and the facility's listing; sent after the first booked
windows ran. The reciprocity rule that defines this letter: **our performance at her
dock is reported FIRST, before her numbers are mentioned** — symmetric measurement was
the pitch, and symmetry that arrives second is hierarchy. Serves both the champion and
the signer (she gets the operational walk-through; the signer gets the same record for
the commercial file — neither bypassed). Zero projections, as ever.*

---

**Subject:** Your first [N] booked windows — our scorecard first, then the record
*(alt: "The windows, the arrivals, and the one we missed")*

Hi **[first name]**,

The first [N] booked windows at [facility] have run — here's the full account,
starting with the part you should grade us on, and fifteen minutes in a quiet window
if you want it walked through. **[Signer]** gets this same record for the file.

**How WE showed up at your doors** *(you're measuring us too — that was the deal)*:
of [N] staged arrivals against booked windows, **[the true numbers — "[x] arrived
inside the window, [y] early by [n] minutes, [z] late — the late one is in the misses
below with its cause"]**. The staging yard exists to absorb the variance your
appointment book can't; where it didn't, that's ours to fix and it's named, not
buried.

**The bookings, at your terms:** [N] windows booked at the schedule you declared.
**[Where it happened: "[n] booking attempts were refused because your declared
capacity was full — the system cannot write an overbook, so your team never had to
bounce one at the door. The refusals you never saw are the feature."]**

**Accessorials during the trial:** **[the true state — "every line item touching your
facility carried its signed evidence; there were [none/n], and each reduced to the
documents" / "no detention events — and here's the part worth noticing: your clean
turns are now VISIBLE clean turns; the record that protects carriers from unverifiable
claims is the same record showing your dock performing, where it counts."]**

**Your measurement basis:** **[where the floor is met: "your facility's numbers now
publish — dwell, appointment integrity — and here is your basis, every signed event
it derives from, yours to inspect and improve."]** **[Where below the floor: "your
numbers don't publish yet — not enough independent visits to clear the contributor
floor, which is the no-single-visit-gotchas protection working; you'll see the basis
the moment it's real."]**

**Payment for the windows:** **[per her card, settled, attested — slips announced
before noticed, same standard as everywhere.]**

**What didn't go right, from us first:**

- **[The honest list — the early trailer that sat on the apron anyway, the booking
  message that arrived late, the driver who missed the door assignment. Each with
  its cause and its consequence. If nothing: the standing luck-not-design humility.]**

**No ask, except the standing one:** keep listing the windows that earn their place;
revise the schedule whenever it should move; delisting is one message and the record
above stays yours. And the thing your docks were promised — showing up in the pricing
as the well-run building you run — is now a matter of record rather than my word.

Thanks for booking the first ones, **[first name]** —

## Usage notes (internal — do not send)

- **Our scorecard comes first, always** — compute the network's arrival performance
  at her dock before drafting; if we were late and the letter leads with her numbers,
  the symmetry pitch is dead. Grade us, then show the record.
- **Overbook refusals framed as the feature** (the coverage-gate framing, dock
  edition): capacity refusals are protection delivered, never demand to apologize
  for or inflate.
- **The k-floor honesty**: if her telemetry hasn't cleared the contributor floor,
  SAY numbers don't publish yet and why — a manager shown "her score" off two visits
  learns the floors were decorative.
- **Both-directions accessorials**: if a detention line hit her facility during the
  trial, it appears with its evidence framing (the shipper letter's symmetry
  paragraph, adapted); if turns were clean, the visible-clean-turns line is the
  pricing-advantage promise starting to be true — state it as record, not
  celebration.
- **Champion and signer both served**: she gets the letter and the walk-through;
  the signer gets the record copy noted in the letter itself — never route around
  her, never leave the signer discovering numbers secondhand.
- **Payment same standard as the yard letter**: external, attested, slips announced
  first.
- **No projections**: next month's windows, volumes, or "growth" never appear —
  the record is the whole letter.
- **Delist handling**: gracious, instant, record handed over — same diner rule as
  the yards; facility people talk to facility people.
