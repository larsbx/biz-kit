# Member redemption follow-up — template (freight co-op internal capital)

*The freight co-op's own member-capital letter — the general case every retiring or
departing member eventually receives, on the same 1B machinery as the service co-op's
(parallel by sovereignty, not duplication: peer co-ops each hold their own documents,
as chapters each hold their own genesis). Sent by the freight co-op's governance when
a member's lifecycle event opens their schedule. **The founding-member section is the
letter's distinct heart**: what redeems is capital; what never redeems — because it
was never capital — is the standing. Series rules throughout.*

---

**Subject:** Your capital account(s) — the accounting, the schedule, and what doesn't leave
*(alt: "What your patronage built: the arithmetic, and the part that stays")*

Dear **[first name]**,

With your **[retirement/departure]** recorded, your capital has moved to redemption.
Everything below recomputes from the record — the same record you've been able to
check every day of your membership, now doing its last job for you the way it did its
first.

**Your account — [or: accounts, one per entity].** **[Where dual membership: "You
held membership in [entity A] and [entity B]; each account accrued separately and
redeems separately — they were never merged, by design, and their schedules run
independently."]** Over [period], your patronage credited **[amount(s)]** — every
credit on the record with the rule that computed it, under weights the members
(you included) declared.

**How it pays:** the standard schedule — **[Y] years, up to [cap] per year, oldest
credits first** — the terms declared before anyone's schedule opened, applied to you
exactly as to everyone. **[First payment: date, amount, method.]** Every payment
lands on the record; a slipping date reaches you before you notice it.

**Why a schedule:** your patronage built things that keep working — the systems, and
in time the yards — for the members still hauling. The sinking fund pays exits
without taxing the people staying; the schedule is that promise's declared, capped
shape. Never a renegotiation, never a favor.

**[FOUNDING-MEMBER SECTION — the part that doesn't leave:]**
**"One more accounting, of the thing that has no schedule because it has no balance:
your founding standing. It was never capital — it can't be redeemed, transferred, or
diminished, and no lifecycle event touches it. The record that says you went first is
append-only like everything else here; it will say so as long as the record exists.
The members you sponsored remain part of that history — their conduct is now theirs
alone, your exposure ended with your membership, but your name in their provenance
never will. The co-op you leave is running on constants you helped write and evidence
you helped gather; that arithmetic doesn't pay out because it never stopped
compounding."**

**What your exit does not touch:** your standing (you leave in good standing, on the
record) · the door back (re-joining is a fresh membership — **[founding: "and your
founding standing greets you at the door; it never left"]**) · and, said plainly:
**if you die mid-schedule, the balance routes to your estate** — machinery, not
goodwill.

**What ends with membership:** your vote. Governance belongs to current members —
the rule you **[founding: "voted for at the first charter session"]** applies to its
authors exactly as to everyone, which is the whole point of it.

**What didn't go right, from us first:**

- **[The honest list — first-schedule friction, named. If nothing: the standing
  humility.]**

**No ask.** The letter closes accounts the right way and sells nothing.
**[Founding: "Thank you for going first — for the hour you gave before there was
anything to show, and for the books that became the demo everyone else believed.
Some of what that built is still yours and arriving on schedule; the rest of it is
everyone's, which was always the design."]**

With respect,

**[FREIGHT CO-OP GOVERNANCE — names/roles]**
**[contact]**

## Usage notes (internal — do not send)

- **Verify every account's arithmetic against the log first** — per entity, never
  merged; the bed-count discipline applied to someone's years.
- **Standard terms only** — the equal-treatment sentence must survive founders
  especially: a founding member's schedule on special terms would convert founding
  standing into exactly the privileged capital the charter forbade (no share
  discounts, no founder economics — 13 §2's exclusions apply at exit as at entry).
- **The founding section is structural fact, not sentiment**: standing permanence =
  append-only record + non-transferable-by-type (13's adversarial suite); the
  sponsorship-legacy line = provenance survives, exposure ends with membership.
  Every sentence in it must be mechanically true or cut.
- **Land-co-op shares are a SEPARATE letter** (the property redemption follow-up) —
  if the departing member also holds shares, both letters apply, neither absorbs
  the other; one machinery each.
- **Tax/paperwork per the membership documents' counsel-defined shape**, with the
  first payment.
- **Estate paragraph stays in; no counter-offers after the lifecycle event;
  slips announced first** — series standards, all of them.
- **The precedent is everyone's**: first member redemption reported to governance
  as aggregate fact (schedule opened, standard terms, on time); individual figures
  stay bilateral.
