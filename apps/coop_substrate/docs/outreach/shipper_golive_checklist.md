# Shipper go-live checklist — the first tendered load

*The other side of the Face's night one — often literally the same load. The
split with `docs/face_golive_checklist.md` is strict: **that checklist owns the
network's gates (authority, ratio, envelopes, split); this one owns HER
evening** — the first tender as the shipper experiences it from her dock, audited
against her one-pager's promises. Its exam: evidence-bound from tender to
invoice means the invoice SHE receives never carries an evidence-free line
either — the symmetry is the product. Its hardest refusal: **the lane is earned,
not bought** — no launch pricing, no loss-leader quote to win her freight, because
a rate that lies at the start is the brokerage model she left. Inherited family
rules: real freight, her declared terms exactly, misses-first tonight.*

---

## 0 — Preconditions

- [ ] `face_golive_checklist.md` running or closed — if her tender IS load one,
      both checklists run tonight, each on its own side; nothing here
      duplicates its gates, and nothing there excuses hers
- [ ] Her onboarding per the scheduling email's manners complete; her lane's
      terms — windows, accessorial schedule, contacts — declared as SHE stated
      them, read back before the tender
- [ ] The quote built at the declared corridor pricing with the margin the
      Face discloses to members — **not a number bent to win the lane**; check
      it against the pricing constants, not against the competitor's teaser
- [ ] Zero volume commitments in the file: her signup asked for none, tonight
      extracts none — confirm no "expected weekly loads" field got filled in
      anywhere on her behalf

## 1 — The tender (her choice, honestly acknowledged)

- [ ] Real freight on her real schedule — no showcase load solicited to
      christen the lane
- [ ] The tender signed onto the rail; her acknowledgment states what is TRUE:
      accepted against declared capacity, not promised against hoped-for
      capacity — if the network can't cover it tonight, she hears that before
      the window, not after (a declined tender handled honestly outranks an
      accepted one handled badly)
- [ ] Her contacts and communication channel per HER declaration — the
      never-anchor manners family, shipper edition: her ops cadence, not ours

## 2 — The carriage (what she sees, and what nobody does)

- [ ] Her load's events visible to her as they land: acceptance, pickup,
      custody, delivery — her own freight's record, live, bilateral
- [ ] **Nothing else of hers is watched**: her other freight, her carriers
      outside this load, her tender patterns — not collected, not analyzed,
      not "insighted"; the no-market-intelligence posture is her protection
      exactly as it is every member's
- [ ] Mid-load exceptions reach her as logged facts with causes, not as
      reassurances — the misses-first register, applied in real time

## 3 — The delivery and the invoice (the promise, on paper)

- [ ] Delivered against the booked window — our arrival number is HER
      scorecard entry, and it starts honest (in or out, stated)
- [ ] **The invoice byte-reproducible, every accessorial line carrying its
      evidence hash or ABSENT** — the symmetry said plainly on invoice one:
      the same rule that lets us bill detention with proof forbids us billing
      it without; she never pays a line she can't verify, starting now
- [ ] Any dispute reduces to comparing documents she already holds — verify
      she HAS them (the delivery record, the condition attestations her load
      generated) before the invoice sends

## 4 — Same-day after

- [ ] Her debrief at her cadence: did the quote hold, did the window hold,
      did the paper match the freight — her answers lead the misses list
- [ ] Misses-first, tender-one edition, written tonight: the pickup that ran
      late, the event that posted slow, the acknowledgment that overstated —
      ours first, in her numbers
- [ ] The weekly status shipper block opens: tenders 1, covered ____,
      in-window ____/1, evidence-free invoice lines 0 (this count stays zero
      forever or the model broke)
- [ ] The shipper trial letter's clock starts (`shipper_trial_followup.md`
      owns the weeks); escalations to the R-queue, assigned not admired

## 5 — What does NOT happen tonight (refusals, verified)

- [ ] **No loss-leader quote** — the lane is earned at the disclosed-margin
      price or not won tonight; a bought lane is a subsidy every member pays
      and a repricing conversation waiting to poison the trial letter
- [ ] No volume ask, tonight or in the follow-up — one tender creates no
      expectation in either direction, and the trial letter will say so too
- [ ] No coverage promised beyond what the folds support — the zero-numbers
      rule facing outward: her acknowledgment quotes declared capacity, never
      enthusiasm
- [ ] No mining of her tender for "pipeline insight" — one load's record is
      one load's record
- [ ] No invoice line without its hash — even (especially) when the detention
      was real and the capture failed; the miss is ours then, not billable
      (the dock's rule, now protecting the other end of the dock)
- [ ] No announcement beyond the folds: one tender, one delivery (in or out
      of window), one clean invoice — counts, not adjectives
