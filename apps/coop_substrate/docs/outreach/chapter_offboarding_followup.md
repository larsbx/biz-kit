# Chapter offboarding follow-up — template (when a chapter dissolves)

*The kit's mirror, at the set's highest altitude: the counterparty is a whole
sovereign co-op. **Sender: the surviving chapter's governance; recipient: the
dissolving chapter's governance and, through them, its members.** The unwind is
the inter-chapter agreement's own Article 8, executed as written — and the letter's
heart is Article 6 outliving the agreement: **we hold your checkpoints, including
your last one, forever**, so your members' history stays verifiable after your
infrastructure goes dark. Non-interference governs to the final sentence: their
wind-down is their charter's, their members are welcomed only as self-directed
individuals through the ordinary gate, and the one absolute this altitude adds is
**the anti-raid rule** — no recruitment campaign into a dissolution, ever. Series
rules; no winback; no annexation.*

---

*(alt: "Article 8, executed as written — and Article 6, which doesn't end")*

To the governance and members of Chapter **[name]**,

With your dissolution **[voted by your membership / triggered per your charter]**
and your signed notice received, this letter executes our side of the agreement
the way both rooms read it aloud at the signing — and records the one clause that
doesn't end.

**[The cause, plainly — pick one:]**
**[Their vote: "Your members decided, which is the entire required explanation —
the sovereignty that made the agreement possible makes the dissolution none of our
business beyond its paperwork."]**
**[Corridor economics: "The corridor's freight thinned until the gates that opened

of it is in the misses below, in numbers."]**
**[Charter-mandated: "Your charter's own trigger fired, and fired visibly — a

**Article 8, executed:**

- **In-flight corridor loads: [completed under the agreement's terms — the list,
  or "none were open."]**
- **Settlement: netted to zero and attested — [the final netting record]. Nothing
  open between any member of yours and any member of ours on this corridor.**
  Open items block this letter.
- **Commons and telemetry grants: ended at termination**, both directions — the
  k-gates traveled with the data while it flowed and the access died with the
  agreement, as signed.

**Article 6, which does not execute because it does not end:** every checkpoint of
yours we ever held, **we keep — including the final one your last signing ceremony
published.** History doesn't un-witness. What that means practically, for any
member of yours holding their export: **they can verify their own records against
the sealed head we hold, forever, without our permission or anyone's** — the
strongest tamper-evidence either of our memberships ever had now protects yours
precisely when your own infrastructure stops running. We ask you to keep our
checkpoints too; the clause was always symmetric, and a dissolved chapter's copy
still witnesses.

**What was never ours, said once so nobody wonders:** your genesis, your log, your
keys, your constants, your members' records — sovereign at the signing, sovereign
at the dissolution. Nothing transfers, merges, or gets absorbed; there is no
machinery by which it could, which both rooms verified the day we signed. Your
internal wind-down — capital accounts, your charter's dissolution provisions —
is yours and your counsel's, and this letter's only word on it is that we have
none.

**Your members, and the rule we bind ourselves to in writing:** any member of
yours is welcome at our gate **as an individual, at their own initiative, through
the ordinary gate — sponsor, standing, verification — carrying their own export**,
which our machinery can read and verify like any member's history. **[Dual members:
"those holding membership in both chapters simply keep ours; the records were never
merged, so nothing needs unwinding."]** And the commitment: **no one from our
chapter will run a recruitment campaign into your dissolution.** A membership drive
aimed at a dissolving chapter is scavenging dressed as welcome; the door is open
and it does not ring.

**What didn't go right across the corridor, from our side first:**

- **[The honest aggregate at corridor scale — the handoffs our members'
  attestations lagged, the checkpoint exchanges we delivered late, the staging
  our end under-filled, the mirrored-custody friction the joint agenda never
  resolved. If the economics variant: our share of the demand shortfall leads,
  in numbers.]**

**The way back, stated once:** if your locality forms again — this cohort or the
next — the chartering kit is where it always was, and a fresh genesis through your
own key ceremony is the whole path; nothing about this dissolution encumbers it.
Nobody will call.

The corridor ran because two sovereign rooms signed the same page and neither
needed the other's permission to exist. That second part is what this letter keeps:
you leave the way you arrived — sovereign, witnessed, and owing nothing.

With respect between chapters,

**[SURVIVING CHAPTER GOVERNANCE — names/roles]**

## Usage notes (internal — do not send)

- **Article 8 verified before drafting**: in-flight loads completed on the record,
  netting attested to zero, grant termination on both logs. Settle-before-send at
  chapter scale — an open cross-corridor obligation makes this letter a dispute
  document, not a goodbye.
- **The final checkpoint MUST be in hand before the letter sends** — request it
  through the signed-notice exchange if the cadence hadn't delivered it; a
  dissolution letter promising to witness a head we don't hold is the letter's
  own tamper story. Verify it (`verify_checkpoint`) on receipt.
- **THE ANTI-RAID RULE IS ABSOLUTE and it binds OUR members, so say it
  internally too**: no outreach lists built from their membership, no "join us"
  messaging into their locality during the wind-down, no sponsor bounty energy —
  sponsorship of an individual who ARRIVES is ordinary and honorable; a campaign
  is predation on a grieving co-op. Standing review treats violations as conduct.
- **Non-interference to the last**: zero language about how they wind down
  capital, who gets what, or what their charter should have said — their organ,
  their counsel, their members. The letter's one sentence on it is a refusal to
  have sentences on it.
- **Individual arrivals get the ordinary gate, verbatim** — sponsor, standing,
  verification; their export verified like any member history (chapters were
  never trust domains for each other's admissions). No bulk anything.
- **Dual members**: confirm on our log that their record with us is untouched by
  the dissolution — per-person sovereignty means their other chapter's death is,
  mechanically, none of our record's business.
- **Misses at corridor scale, ours first** — the mirrored-attestation lag is
  almost certainly on the list; the 09 §1 federation-events question the joint
  agenda never resolved gets named honestly if it bit.
- **The precedent is constitutional and BILATERAL**: the first dissolution
  executed exactly per Article 8 is every future chapter's evidence that the
  exit clause was real — report to our governance as aggregate; offer the
  dissolving governance the same summary for their final records.
- **Re-chartering: the kit, fresh genesis, their ceremony** — never "reactivation"
  of the old chapter_id; a genesis is a birth, not a reboot (the kit's TOFU
  discipline). The door-doesn't-ring rule at chapter scale.
