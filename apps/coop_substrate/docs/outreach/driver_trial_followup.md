# Driver trial follow-up — template (after the first relay legs)

*Sent after a driver's first weeks running relay legs for a member carrier. The
letter's currency was set before it existed: **the count of beds** — home-daily was
pitched as geometry, so the follow-up is arithmetic. Its hardest boundary is
constitutional: **his pay and employment are his and his carrier's** (00 Art. II) —
this letter accounts for the geometry, his stake, and the network's conduct, and never
crosses that wall, even to sympathize. Assumes he's a driver-member; bracket where
he isn't yet. Zero volume promises.*

---

**Subject:** Your first [N] relay legs — the bed count, and the two nights we owe
an explanation for
*(alt: "Home-daily, measured: here's the arithmetic")*

Hi **[first name]**,

You've run [N] legs on the [corridor] relay — here's the accounting, starting with
the only number the whole design was drawn against.

**The bed count:** of [N] leg-days, **you slept at home [X].** Every leg assigned to
you fit inside the declared daily range — the system can't assign one that doesn't —
so the [Y] nights that went wrong anyway are the misses below, each with its cause,
because a leg that fits on paper and still costs you a night out is OUR variance to
explain, not yours to absorb quietly.

**What didn't go right, from us first:**

- **[The honest list, night by night — "the [date] leg fit the range but the hook at
  [yard] ran late by [n] hours: dwell exception, logged, and here's what changed" ·
  the reassignment that stretched a day · the yard queue that ate the margin. Each
  named, each with its consequence. If all nights were home: "none — [N] for [N],
  which after a few weeks I credit to the geometry AND to luck; the first miss will
  lead the next letter."]**

**Your stake, accruing:** **[member: "your relay participation is crediting your
capital account under the weights the charter set — it's live on your phone, every
credit showing the rule that made it; that's the 'own a stake' line from the ad,
being arithmetic instead of adjective."]** **[Not yet a member: "the membership
conversation stands open at your pace — nothing about driving relay requires it,
and nothing about declining it changes your legs."]**

**The waiting question — your design input, still wanted:** you told us what you're
told while you wait was half the indignity. How did the relay do — at the yards,
between legs? What you report shapes the next revision, and **network conduct is ours
to answer for: if a yard or a dispatch decision treated you poorly, that's
standing-review input here.** What happens inside your employment — pay, hours, the
cab — stays yours and your carrier's; the constitution keeps us out of it, which
protects you as much as them.

**The graduation path, status verbatim:** **[where true: "the lease-to-own instrument
remains exactly where I said it was — designed, written, and not offered until
lawyers clear it for your state. Nothing has changed, so nothing is being offered.
When that sentence changes, you'll hear it as terms you can read, not as a pitch."]**
**[Where a state cleared: counsel-approved language only.]**

**No ask.** Keep running the legs that get you home; tell me about the ones that
don't. The relay earns your seat-time or it doesn't deserve it.

Thanks for driving the first ones, **[first name]** —

**[FOUNDER — name]**
**[phone]**

## Usage notes (internal — do not send)

- **Compute the bed count from the log before drafting** — leg events, home-terminal
  arrivals, the arithmetic checked. A wrong bed count in this letter is worse than
  no letter; it's the one number he already knows.
- **Every night out gets a cause** — dwell exceptions, reassignments, yard queues,
  from the logged events. "Variance happens" is not a cause; the specific exception
  is. The line "OUR variance to explain, not yours to absorb quietly" is the
  letter's contract — honor it or cut it.
- **The employment wall, absolute**: pay, hours, home-time policy, anything between
  him and his carrier — never discussed, never sympathized into ("that sounds
  rough" about pay is a foot over the wall). If he raises employment grievances:
  the boundary sentence from the letter, verbatim, plus genuine warmth. If he
  reports NETWORK conduct (yard treatment, dispatch fairness), take it — that's
  standing-review input (04 §2) and the fairness floor's business (04 §5 I3).
- **Graduation status: the not-yet held verbatim** until counsel's cleared language
  exists for his state — the driver one-pager's "anyone offering you one today
  isn't us" still governs, including us.
- **Stake talk only for members**, pointed at his own view with rule ids — never
  projected forward, never converted to recruitment pressure on non-members.
- **No leg-volume promises**, no next-month talk — the relay is corridor-dependent
  and the letter says only what ran.
- **Never send while he's rolling** — the scheduling email's rule applies to the
  follow-up call too; the letter can land anytime, the conversation waits for
  parked.
