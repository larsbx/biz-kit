# Driver go-live checklist — the first solo dispatch

*The corridor go-live scripted a NIGHT that was new; this one scripts a DRIVER
who is — his first dispatch alone through the network, on whatever corridor,
mature or not (if his first solo happens to be a corridor's first leg, run both
checklists; they own different things). Its two centers: **the activation chain
is real before any tender reaches him** — his qualifications verified through
the declared chain, visibly, never vouched past — and **his first signature is
not his first exposure**: the custody pattern was walked through with him at
onboarding, so night one tests his driving, not his paperwork literacy. The
absolutes travel with the persona: never while rolling, the employment wall,
his yes genuinely his. Bracketed throughout: **[OO]** items for the graduated
owner-operator whose first solo is also his first load under his OWN
authority.*

---

## 0 — Preconditions (him, verified — not vouched)

- [ ] **The activation verification chain COMPLETE on the record**: CDL,
      medical, and the rest of the declared chain (the 3.6 discipline) —
      verified through the chain's own gates, not through anyone's confidence
      in him; the gates are visible, and he can see his own passed
- [ ] His membership state current; his acceptance channel set up and tested
      (his phone, HIS terms for contact — and the contact rule on file: who
      calls whom, parked only)
- [ ] **The signature walkthrough DONE before tonight**: he has signed a
      practice custody pair (hook and drop) on the test log, seen his own
      signature verify, and knows what he's attesting when he signs a
      condition line — a driver learning the pattern at a dark yard gate at
      22:00 is a failure of ours that will read as a failure of his
- [ ] The assignment gate's inputs true for HIM: HOS feasibility from the
      declared source; if a relay leg, the leg fits the declared daily_range —
      the system can't assign otherwise, and tonight he's shown the check
      passing (his theorem, inherited)
- [ ] **[OO]** The class transition on the log (driver → owner-operator, his
      stake continuous), HIS carrier envelope signed (his lanes, his floors,
      his ceiling), tenders routing to HIS authority — read the routing back;
      the graduation letter's "what changes now" is tonight's mechanics
- [ ] **[Fleet driver]** His carrier's envelope covers the load; his
      employment terms stay where they live — behind the wall; tonight
      involves his employer's paper only where the load's routing needs it

## 1 — The dispatch (an offer, briefed)

- [ ] Real freight — no training load invented to look like work
- [ ] The offer explicit, his decline costless, said in words on dispatch one
      (the constitution's voluntariness, at the moment it's most tempting to
      assume)
- [ ] The briefing he actually needs: the yards on his route and their rules,
      who signs at each end, where he stages if early, who he calls PARKED if
      anything goes sideways — the plan for mid-run trouble made before he
      rolls, because it cannot be made while he rolls

## 2 — The run (his driving, our silence)

- [ ] Origin custody: his first real dual-signed hook — the walked-through
      pattern, now live; his copy of the condition report in his hands like
      any counterparty's
- [ ] **Nothing while rolling** — no check-in, no "just confirming," no ETA
      nudge; the deleted node stays deleted for his first solo exactly as for
      the corridor's; exceptions wait at the next parked event
- [ ] Destination custody: the drop dual-signed, the chain complete — his
      first end-to-end signed load on the record

## 3 — The night's numbers (his, starting honest)

- [ ] If a relay leg: his bed — home or not, counted from the record, the
      miss (if any) named tonight with its cause; his personal bed count
      starts as honestly as the corridor's did
- [ ] His stake, if member: patronage from tonight credited with the rule id
      visible in his own view — "own a stake" as arithmetic on night one
      **[non-member: nothing accrues, nothing is implied; the membership
      conversation stays at his pace, unlinked to his driving]**
- [ ] **[OO]** His first load's economics under his own authority visible to
      him same-day — tender, rate, settlement state — the open-book he
      graduated into, working on load one

## 4 — Same-day (parked) after

- [ ] His debrief, parked, at his pace: the dispatch's accuracy, the yards'
      treatment of him, the waiting — network conduct is ours to fix; his
      employment (if fleet) stays behind the wall in the debrief exactly as
      everywhere
- [ ] Misses-first, solo-one edition, written tonight: the briefing gap he
      hit, the gate that didn't know he was coming, the signature that took
      three tries because our walkthrough missed a step — ours first
- [ ] The weekly status driver block: first solos 1, chains complete 1/1,
      beds ____ (where relay), rolling contacts 0 (this count stays zero or
      the rule broke)
- [ ] The driver trial letter's clock starts (`driver_trial_followup.md` owns
      the weeks); escalations to the R-queue, assigned not admired

## 5 — What does NOT happen tonight (refusals, verified)

- [ ] No tender to an incomplete activation chain — "he's obviously qualified"
      is vouching, and the chain exists because vouching is how corners get
      cut on exactly the nights that matter
- [ ] No call to the cab — first-solo nerves are OURS to manage, not his to
      absorb as interruptions
- [ ] No assignment outside range or HOS "just this once, he's fresh" — the
      gate has no launch-night exception, and he's shown that it held FOR him
- [ ] No employment questions, no pay talk, no "how's the fleet treating you"
      in any debrief — the wall, from night one
- [ ] No membership pitch stapled to his dispatch — driving and joining are
      separate conversations at separate paces, tonight and always
- [ ] No announcement beyond the folds: one solo, one complete chain, zero
      rolling contacts — counts, not adjectives
