# Interview scheduling email — template

*Companion to `friendly_carrier_onepager.md` (runbook Phase 1→2 handoff). Send 1:1,
after a personal conversation — never as a blast (corpus 12 §4: relationship mail, not
sequence mail; the compliance-envelope machinery for bulk outreach is a later brief and
deliberately unbuilt). Same discipline as the one-pager: no numbers, no promises,
mechanism language only (12 §3, 13A). Fill the brackets; cut anything, add nothing
quantitative.*

---

## Usage notes (internal — do not send)

- **Precondition:** they've already heard the pitch from you personally. This template
  schedules; it does not introduce.
- **Recording:** before offering it at all, confirm the recording-consent statute for
  *their* state (runbook 0.1). In two-party states where you haven't checked, delete
  bullet 2's offer framing and keep notes-only.
- **The document ask** stays soft ("entirely optional") — collection happens under their
  consent classes on the day, via `mix harness.document`, never over email. If they
  email a document anyway, treat it as uncollected until consent is signed.
- **No calendar-tool tracking pixels or link shorteners** — this relationship runs on
  the same candor the system does.
- **Decline handling:** one thank-you reply, no follow-up sequence. A "no" here is
  provenance too — note it privately; nothing goes on the log about someone who didn't
  consent to be on it.

  exist) · 1:1-not-blast → corpus 12 §4 · "nothing to sell" → repo state (gate(D)
  false, no dispatch build).
