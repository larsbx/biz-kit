## Usage notes (internal — do not send)

- **Around her calls, enforced by you**: mid-hour call = she takes it, you rebook
  cheerfully. The mechanic whose interview cost her a job remembers it exactly the
  way the shop owner would.
- **Employment hygiene**: her fleet relationship is untouched and unmentioned beyond
  what she volunteers. If she asks "should I quit for this," the honest answer is the
  formation truth: there's nothing to quit FOR yet — the co-op charters first, counsel
  clears the paperwork per state, and founding worker-members will have read
  everything before anything is signed. Nobody should leave a paycheck for a design
  document.
- **The fleet-capital question is genuinely open** (buy-in vs earned-in vs loan) and
  it's the FOUNDERS' to decide — present it as her potential decision, never as
  settled policy she'd be joining.
- **Classification "not yet" verbatim** where true — this industry's 1099 scars mean
  any vagueness reads as the old game. The one-pager's line is the script: "if those
  documents don't exist yet for your state, the honest answer is 'not yet,' and
  you'll hear it in those words."
- **Consent ceremony standard** (per the script); remote variant per the driver
  email's mechanics if she prefers video — seed never via SMS/email.
- **Recording**: normal 0.1 state check — she's local, so unlike OTR this is a clean
  yes/no from counsel's list; unchecked state = notes-only, delete the ask.
- **Decline handling**: one thank-you, park it, no sequence — and her 2 a.m.
  availability to whoever calls her today stays exactly what it was; the network
  never inserts itself into arrangements that predate it.
