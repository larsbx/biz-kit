## What this is

The cooperative's land arm: yards and lots the freight network actually uses, held by
a member-owned land co-op — **title to real ground you can drive past**, not paper
that needs a decoder. Shares in it are the way a member's savings can own a piece of
that ground, under rules built for exactly the person who has refused every
interest-bearing thing a bank ever offered them.

## How the share actually works (arithmetic, not narrative)

- **Share value = appraised assets, plus cash, minus liabilities, over shares
  outstanding.** Independent appraisal, recorded with its provenance; a disputed
  appraisal gets a second appraiser by declared protocol. No market sentiment in the
  number — there is no market (below, on purpose).
- **You buy in and redeem at that value, with the co-op as the only counterparty.**
  Selling shares to a third party isn't forbidden — it's *unrepresentable*; the system
  cannot record such a transfer. No flipping, no speculation, no whale accumulating
  quietly: a per-member ceiling (κ) is in the charter and in the code.
- **No interest anywhere in the structure — by construction.** The instruments this
  co-op may use to acquire land form a closed list (share capital, contribution at
  appraisal, declining co-ownership, cost-plus resale, lease-to-own, a benevolent
  bridge for timing) — an interest-bearing liability is not on the list and cannot be
  written onto it quietly. That's a property of the contract types, not a promise.
- **The co-op cannot buy on a hunch.** Acquisitions are evidence-gated: a parcel is
  bought only after realized leasing income has proven the demand — the gates are
  computed from the record, and enthusiasm is not an input.

## The exit, declared before the entrance

Redemption follows **liquidity gates declared before you ever buy**, first-in
first-out, and exit is **without forfeiture** — leaving costs you standing in line,
never a haircut for the sin of leaving. Read the gates first; they are short, and they
are the honest price of owning ground instead of paper.

## Your vote

One member, one vote — your shares scale your economic participation, **never your
say**. The twenty-share member and the ceiling-share member vote identically, and the
ceiling exists so that sentence stays true.

## The risk paragraph — read it because it's true *(this is the body, not the fine print)*

Appraisals move, and **share value can fall as well as rise**. Redemptions follow the
declared gates **and may queue** — this is not a demand deposit. **Income depends on
utilization and is not guaranteed** — lots earn when trucks stage, and trucks stage
when corridors run. Shares are **not deposits, not insured, and have no public
market** — the no-market rule that protects the structure also means your exit is the
redemption process, at NAV, in line. Return without risk is *forbidden* in this
co-op's constitution — so if the risks above were softened anywhere on this page, the
whole thing would be a lie about its own foundations. They aren't, and it isn't.

## What we will never do

Promise yields · create a trading market (unrepresentable, above) · let any member
exceed the ceiling · acquire ahead of evidence · touch your money before you've read
the gates, the appraisal basis, and this paragraph — and had time to walk away.

first (it's how everything here starts), shares only when and as the counsel-cleared
