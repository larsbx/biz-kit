# Shop-owner trial follow-up — template (after the first referrals)

*Follows the walkthrough and his capability listing; sent after the first referred
jobs ran through his bays. Two things make this letter unlike its siblings: **the
money section reports an absence** — the network routed need, but every invoice was
his and the member's, untouched, which was the promise; and **the reciprocity runs
through conduct** — how members treated his bays is standing-review input he's
invited (never required) to give. Our scorecard first, as always: here it's match
quality. Zero projections.*

---

**Subject:** Your first [N] referrals — how our matching did, and what we never touched
*(alt: "The first jobs through — our misroutes first")*

Hi **[first name]**,

The first [N] network referrals have been through your bays — here's the account,
starting with the part you should grade us on. Twenty minutes over coffee if you'd
rather talk it than read it (around your bays, same as always).

**How OUR matching did** *(this is the part that's ours to answer for)*: [N] jobs
routed against the capabilities you declared. **[The true state — "every one matched
what you listed" / "one was outside your list — [the misroute] — that's our matching
missing, not your shop failing, and here's what changed in the routing."]** You
declined [n] — noted without comment, because your yes was always yours.

**The promise about your calendar:** the system's picture of your availability came
from your own work orders, and **[where it happened: "[n] referrals were held back
because your queue showed full — the holds you never saw are the feature; nobody got
promised a bay you didn't have."]** No double-bookings. **[If one occurred: it leads
the misses list, with what changed.]**

**What's absent from this letter, on purpose: your money.** Every job the network
routed was invoiced by you, to the member, at your price — we never saw it, never
touched it, never will. **[Where group purchasing is live: "the exception you can
check: parts through the buying program, where the spread is the audited number you
were promised — here's the view."]**

**Your track record, beginning:** your finished jobs are now signed events — the
reputation that routes future work. **[Above the floor: "your numbers publish; here's
your basis, every job it derives from."]** **[Below: "not enough independent jobs yet
to clear the publication floor — no score exists, which is the no-gotchas protection
working; you'll see the basis the moment it's real."]**

**And the part that's yours to tell us:** how the members treated your shop — paid
promptly, showed up, respected the bay? That's conduct-standing input in this network:
**a member who wastes your Saturday answers for it the same way anyone answers for
conduct here.** Tell me anything worth telling; it goes where it has consequences,
or nowhere, your call.

**What didn't go right, from us first:**

- **[The honest list — the misroute above in full, the referral message that arrived
  garbled, the member who no-showed (and its standing consequence). If nothing: the
  series' standing luck-not-design humility.]**

**No ask, except the standing one:** keep the listing if the flow earns its place;
update your capabilities as your bays change (your declaration, one message);
membership stays a separate conversation at your pace; delisting is instant and
changes nothing about who calls you directly.

Thanks for taking the first ones, **[first name]** —

**[FOUNDER — name]**

## Usage notes (internal — do not send)

- **Our scorecard = match quality**: audit every referral against his declared
  capabilities BEFORE drafting; a misroute he noticed that we didn't name kills the
  letter. Declines are reported without commentary — his yes is the envelope logic
  at shop scale.
- **The money-absence paragraph is the letter's spine** — it's the anti-marketplace
  promise kept, and it must be literally true: if any mechanism touched his revenue,
  this letter is wrong and the mechanism is the problem.
- **Queue holds framed as the feature** (the refusal-as-protection pattern, shop
  edition).
- **K-floor honesty identical to the dock letter** — no score off two jobs.
- **The conduct invitation is real machinery**: his reports feed standing review
  (04 §2) — invite once, take what he gives, never require. If a member no-showed
  or slow-paid him, the consequence is named in the misses (like the yard letter's
  driver) — his rules being enforceable on our side, shop edition.
- **Group purchasing spread view only where live** — otherwise the designed-and-
  dormant honesty line, not a promise.
- **No projections, no volume talk** — flow follows member demand; the record is
  the letter.
- **Delist handling**: instant, gracious, and explicitly severed from his direct
  relationships — members keep calling whoever they trust, before, during, after.
