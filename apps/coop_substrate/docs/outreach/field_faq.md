# Field FAQ — what carriers actually ask, and answers that stay honest

*Companion to the one-pager and ceremony scripts. Same discipline throughout (corpus
12 §3, 13A): mechanism language, zero numbers, launch-state qualifiers. Each answer is a
suggested spoken shape — your voice, these facts. The universal fallback is always
compliant: **"That's not decided yet — and here's who gets to decide it"** (usually: the
members, in the charter, one vote each). Never improvise a number. Never log who asked
what.*

---

**"Is this an MLM? Am I going to be asked to recruit?"**
> "No — and not as a policy, as a structure: the software literally cannot record a
> recruitment payment. Nobody earns a dime for bringing names. If you ever invite
> someone, what you take on is responsibility for them, not a commission."
*(13 §5 anti-pyramid predicate; registry absence test.)*

**"What does it cost me?"**
> "Today: an hour. There's nothing to buy and I wouldn't take your money if you offered.
> Later, membership will have dues — set in the charter by the founding members, so if
> you're in the room, you're setting them."
*(13 §2; dues = fee-for-service, 04 §4; constants member-declared.)*

**"Are you a broker? Are you after my customers?"**
> "I'm not, and no. Your customers, your lanes, your contacts are yours by
> constitution — the system can't even ask for them; sharing anything is a specific,
> signed choice you'd make per item. Down the road the co-op runs its own brokerage —
> open-book to members, meaning the owners see the spread — and using it is voluntary,
> load by load."
*(00 Art. II.3; 04 §5 I2/I7; edges sovereign, 07 §5.)*

**"So this thing dispatches my trucks? Replaces my dispatcher?"**
> "Never. The constitution's second article: the co-op never controls your equipment,
> never dispatches your power, never touches your drivers. What the tool does is the
> paperwork around decisions **you** already make — and it only ever acts inside limits
> you write and sign yourself. That's actually the interview's big question: what would
> you let software say yes to, and where does trust stop? Your answer becomes your
> setting."
*(00 Art. II.4; H5 envelopes, 10 §2; the co-3 question.)*

**"Who sees my rates and my numbers?"**
> "You, and whoever you're already doing the deal with — same as today. The system is
> built so no member ever sees another member's detail; that's not a settings page,
> it's tested into the software. Anything shared wider is a pooled total with your
> numbers invisible inside it."
*(No-surveillance classification test; bilateral disclosure; Aggregate seam.)*

**"What's this Islamic finance thing? I'm not Muslim."**
> "Membership's open to anyone who deals straight — the rules are about the *money's*
> conduct, not yours. Practically they mean: no interest-bearing instruments anywhere,
> no betting on freight, every term written and signed before it binds, and no fine
> print that reconstructs any of that through the back door. Most carriers hear that
> list and say 'so, how business used to work.'"
*(00 Art. III; 05 closed sets; admission = conduct standing, 04 §2.)*

**"When does it launch?"**
> "When enough working carriers have told us what to build — and not before. That's a
> hard rule in the software: the build is locked behind the interviews. Right now the
> ledger and the rulebook run; the dispatch tool doesn't exist yet, on purpose. Your
> hour is literally what unlocks it or kills it."
*(gate(D), corpus 11 §0/§2; repo state.)*

**"What do YOU get out of this?"**
> **[FOUNDER — your true answer, in your words. The structural backstop you can add:]**
> "And whatever the co-op ever pays whoever runs the back office, it's a declared wage
> or fee the members vote — never a cut of the freight. Skimming is the thing this
> exists to end, so it's built to be impossible to do quietly."
*(04 §7: operator comp fee-or-salary, never a spread; rent-resolution, 00 Art. IV.4.)*

**"Who else is doing this? Who's in?"**
> "I don't name people who haven't said I could — same protection you're getting right
> now. What I can tell you is the shape: owner-operators and small fleets, five to
> eight of them for this round, people whose word is good."
*(Bilateral discipline; 13 §1 cohort shape. Never name interviewees.)*

**"Do I HAVE to run my freight through it later?"**
> "No stage of this is ever mandatory — that's constitutional. It's an offer, not a
> fence; if the numbers don't work for you on a given load, you go around it and
> nobody blinks."
*(00 Art. II.5: bypass permitted; I7 voluntary routing.)*

**"One vote each — even if I run twenty trucks and he runs one?"**
> "One member, one vote, always — money buys more economic participation, never more
> say. That's the article most everything else hangs off, and it's the one thing I'd
> tell you nobody will ever successfully change quietly: changes to the rules are
> themselves signed, public-to-members events."
*(00 Art. IV.1; Art. VII amendment discipline.)*

**"What happens to my interview if this whole thing dies?"**
> "Your words stay under the permissions you gave and nothing else — there's no company
> to sell a database to and no database worth selling; your detail was never in a pile
> with everyone else's. And the take-it-back key works whether the project is alive or
> dead."
*(Consent classes; bilateral streams; revocation mechanics.)*

**"Can I see it actually work?"**
> "Right now, on this laptop. Watch —" *(the consent demo from the ceremony script:
> throwaway consent, revoke it, show the counts refuse it)* "— that's the machinery
> keeping a promise with me trying to break it."
*(mix tasks, live; ceremony script step 5.)*

**"What's the catch?"**
> "Three honest ones. It's early — you'd be shaping a thing, not buying a thing. There
> are no guarantees of rates, savings, or timelines, and anyone who promises you those
> today is lying. And it'll ask for patience: everything here moves at the speed of
> evidence, which is slower than a sales pitch and the entire point."
*(13A risk register; 12 §3; capital-follows-evidence, 03.)*

## Usage notes (internal)

- If a question has no entry here: the not-decided-yet fallback, plus *where* it gets
  decided. Guessing generates a claim you can't back; the corpus treats unresolvable
  claims as build failures, and your mouth is a build surface (12 P1).
- Questions about **insurance, lease-to-own, land shares**: real programs in the design,
  all counsel-gated — "designed, written down, and waiting on lawyers before I'm
  allowed to pitch it" is the complete answer. Do not elaborate benefits ([SECURITIES]/
  [LEGAL] wrappers, 13A).
- New question worth keeping? Add it here with a provenance line — this file is an
  instrument like the question tree: versioned in git, drift visible.
