# Friendly-carrier recruitment tracker — template

*Copy into your **private notes directory** — never committed, never on the log. This is
the one place in the whole project where pre-consent people are written down, and the
promises in the ceremony scripts depend on it staying private: a person who declines
never becomes data anywhere else, and once someone consents, THIS SHEET is also the only
place their name meets their `IV-ref` — which makes it exactly as sensitive as your keys
directory. Guard it the same way.*

*What this sheet is NOT: a substitute for the log. From consent onward, `mix
harness.status` and the folds are the tracker — duplicate nothing here that the system
derives. This sheet exists only for the stretch the corpus calls relationship work, not
spec work (13 §8.2), which no fold can see.*

## Parked (declined or cold — the discipline)

|---|---|---|---|
| | | ✗ / ~ | — |

*A "✗" means: one thank-you already sent, never contact about this again, and the only
thing written down is "stop asking." A "~" (cold) may be warmly re-approached ONCE if
circumstances genuinely change — your judgment, no sequence, no scheduling. If someone
asks you to forget them entirely, delete their row; this sheet honors deletion even
though the log can't need to (they were never on it).*

## Weekly self-check (five questions, thirty seconds)

```
[ ] Is anyone stuck in "scheduled" > 2 weeks? (re-confirm once, then park as cold)
[ ] Does persona coverage still reach all three? (recruit toward the gap, not the easy yes)
[ ] Any new state in the pipeline that counsel hasn't cleared for recording?
[ ] Is every "next action" dated, and past-dated ones done or re-dated honestly?
[ ] Have I written anything here that belongs on the log instead — or vice versa?
```
