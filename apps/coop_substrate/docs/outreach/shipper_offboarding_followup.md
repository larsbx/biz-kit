# Shipper offboarding follow-up — template (when a lane winds down)

*Sent when the relationship winds down — by her choice (she stopped tendering, which
was always the exit), by the corridor's (evidence faded, gates closed it the way it
woke), or by drift. The register differs by cause — **hers gets pure grace; the
corridor's gets our explanation** — but the spine is the same: final accounting, the
complete verifiable archive as the going-away gift, her data's fate stated exactly,
and the winback silence. **This letter removes her from every future contact
expectation — the no-re-pitch rule's final form: no drip campaign survives it.***

---

**Subject:** Closing the [lane] file — your complete records, and a clean goodbye
*(alt: "The lane's wind-down: final accounting and your archive")*

Hi **[first name]**,

**[The cause, plainly — pick one:]**
**[Her choice: "With your freight moved elsewhere, this letter closes the file the
way we opened it — with the receipts. No questions asked about the why; the exit was
always yours, and it was always going to look exactly like this."]**
**[Corridor deactivation: "The [corridor] wound down — the evidence that activated it
faded, and the same gates that opened it closed it automatically; that's the design
working, not a decision made about you. Your in-flight loads completed under the
terms they started with, as promised at the first session. This letter closes the
file properly."]**

**The final accounting:** **[the true state — "every invoice settled; nothing open
between us" — an open item BLOCKS this letter; see notes.]**

**Your archive — the going-away gift with teeth:** attached is the complete custody
export for every load you ever ran through this network — every signed touch, dock to
dock, with the verification instructions. **It verifies offline, forever, with nothing
from us**: your auditors can check any of it in ten years whether or not this co-op
still answers the phone. That property was the product on day one; it's yours to keep
on the last day too.

**Your data's fate, exactly:** what the transactions themselves created — the signed
custody and invoice records — is permanent, bilateral, and sealed: visible to you and
to the parties of each load, never pooled, never sold, never becoming anyone's market
intelligence, and this doesn't change because the relationship ended. Beyond those
records: nothing is retained. You come off every contact list this letter's sending
implies — **no quarterly check-ins, no win-back sequence, no newsletter. If you ever
want us, you know where the desk is; it will not be calling you.**

**Your credit line:** dormant, not destroyed — if freight ever comes back this way,
tenders resume without a second ceremony **[per policy: subject to an ordinary credit
refresh if the line has sat cold]**. The quote will still be live and the receipts
will still be the product; that's a fact, not a pitch.

**The whole-relationship scorecard, one last time:** over [N] loads — **[the honest
aggregate: on-time performance, the disputes and how each reduced to documents, the
misses from every trial letter that stayed fixed or didn't. Ours to state, hers to
keep.]**

Thank you for judging us on the receipts, start to finish, **[first name]** — it made
us better at exactly the rate your audits demanded.

**[FACE OPERATOR — name]**
**[contact]**

## Usage notes (internal — do not send)

- **Open items block the letter**: every invoice settled, every dispute closed or
  explicitly carried into a separate resolution track — never a warm goodbye over an
  open argument (the trial letter's rule, final application).
- **The archive self-verified before sending** — the full-history export run through
  verification yourself; the ten-year promise is the letter's spine and it must be
  true the day it's made.
- **The contact-expectation removal is EXECUTED, not stated**: whatever list, tickler,
  or habit would have prompted future outreach — cleared the day this sends. The
  letter's promise of silence is machinery-grade or it's marketing.
- **Cause variants kept honest**: her choice gets zero probing ("no questions asked"
  means none — not even a feedback survey); the corridor's gets the deactivation
  explained as the evidence-gate design (corridor checklist item 9), never as
  circumstances beyond anyone's control — it was precisely within the design's
  control, which is the point.
- **Dormant-line mechanics accurate per current policy** — don't promise
  no-second-ceremony if the credit process would actually require one after [n]
  months; check before writing.
- **The scorecard is aggregate and final** — misses included, especially the ones
  that never got fixed; a goodbye letter that airbrushes is the last impression and
  the worst one.
- **The diner rule, shipper edition**: logistics people talk to logistics people —
  the clean exit IS the marketing, and treating it as marketing would ruin it.
  Send the letter for her, not for the story she'll tell; she'll tell it anyway.
