# Mechanic go-live checklist — the first service call

*A sixth first night, with one difference that governs everything: **it isn't
ours to run.** The service co-op is a sovereign worker co-op; this checklist is a
gifted template — like the mechanic letters — that ITS governance executes, in
its own name, under its own charter. The freight co-op's only role tonight is
being the network whose member broke down. The night's exam is double: the
member gets fixed at the declared card, and **the worker's protections hold on
the very first call** — her paperwork signed before her boots hit a yard, her
acceptance genuinely hers at the moment dispatch first pings, her patronage
crediting under a rule that existed before the wrench turned. Inherited family
rules: real breakdown, no showcase; refusals verified; misses-first written
tonight.*

---

## 0 — Preconditions (the worker co-op's own gates)

- [ ] The co-op chartered; the classification and membership paperwork —
      counsel-reviewed per its charter (Tier 3.4) — **SIGNED before the first
      call**: "nothing improvised at the entrance" is the promise her
      offboarding letter will one day repeat; it starts being true tonight or
      it never was
- [ ] The rate card DECLARED on the log — the card governs the co-op's
      dispatch (and only that: her phone was never the card's business); read
      it back before the first job quotes from it
- [ ] Her patronage rule active BEFORE the call: kinds and weights declared —
      a job worked before the weight exists cannot accrue, and retroactive
      crediting is retro-fit, which is void (the session-two discipline,
      worker co-op edition)
- [ ] The truck and tools issued with a **signed condition report at
      handover** — the same one-pattern that will protect her at return
      protects the fleet from day one; her offboarding letter assumes this
      report exists, so it has to
- [ ] The dispatch channel rehearsed on the test log: need → offer →
      acceptance → completion, green before real freight is stranded behind it

## 1 — The call (real, and hers to take)

- [ ] A real breakdown on its real clock — no staged job to christen the truck
- [ ] Dispatch through the CO-OP's channel at the declared card — never
      through her personal phone; the boundary between the co-op's dispatch
      and her own line is drawn on call one, because it's the boundary her
      exit letter will one day stand on
- [ ] **The offer is an offer**: her acceptance explicit, her decline costless —
      said in words on the first ping, and meant at 2 a.m. as much as at
      2 p.m.; the call is answered by her choice, which is what makes her
      answering it worth something
- [ ] The member told the honest shape: the co-op's mechanic, at the declared
      card, invoiced by the co-op — no surprise economics at the roadside

## 2 — The work (her competence, her scope)

- [ ] Scope is her call at the vehicle — nobody dispatches a diagnosis; the
      card prices the work, the worker judges it
- [ ] Safety floor absolute: a roadside she judges unsafe gets moved or
      towed — no job completes on bravado, and the co-op's paper says so
      before its first job tests it
- [ ] The completion dual-signed — mechanic and member — outcome factual: the
      first brick of the co-op's record, and of hers

## 3 — The money (the co-op's, the member's, and hers — three clean lines)

- [ ] The invoice: the co-op's, at the card, to the member — settled
      externally, attested on the rail; no fund movement on anyone's platform
- [ ] **Her patronage credited same-day, with the rule id on the credit** —
      the "own a stake" line becoming arithmetic on job one, visible in her
      own view
- [ ] Her pay per the chartered terms — the co-op's internal business, named
      here only as: on time, per the documents she signed, with any slip
      announced before she notices it

## 4 — Same-day after

- [ ] Her debrief, at her pace: did the dispatch respect her yes? Was the
      call's information accurate? Would she take call two? — her answers
      lead the misses list
- [ ] The member's debrief separately: fixed? At the card? — bilateral,
      factual
- [ ] Misses-first, call-one edition, written tonight: the ping that came in
      badly, the location that was wrong, the part that wasn't on the truck
- [ ] The co-op's own cadence opens its first report: calls 1, accepted ____,
      completed ____, card honored 1/1, patronage credited 1/1 — ITS report,
      to ITS members; the freight co-op reads nothing but the bilateral
      records it's party to
- [ ] Escalations: network-side conduct (a yard that blocked her, a dispatch
      that misfired) to the freight co-op's R-queue; co-op-internal matters
      to its own governance — two rails, never crossed

## 5 — What does NOT happen tonight (refusals, verified)

- [ ] No call before the paperwork — a stranded truck does not compress the
      classification honesty; the member waits or another shop gets the
      referral, and the co-op's first no is its charter meaning itself
- [ ] No dispatch to her personal phone, tonight or ever — the channel
      boundary has no launch-night exception
- [ ] No acceptance pressure, no "we really need this one" — her costless
      decline is the constitution's voluntariness applied to the person the
      network will most want to lean on
- [ ] No scope dictated from the dispatch desk — the card prices, the worker
      judges
- [ ] No employment-shaped improvisation: nothing paid, promised, or asked
      outside the chartered documents — the wall that protects the
      classification protects her
- [ ] No announcement beyond the folds: one call, one completion, card
      honored, stake credited — counts, not adjectives
