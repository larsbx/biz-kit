# Driver offboarding follow-up — template (when a driver departs)

*Scoped tightly, because most things a driver stops doing are NOT departures:
stopping relay legs is just the voluntary network being voluntary (no letter);
changing employers doesn't touch his membership, which was never his carrier's to
carry (no letter — and if his fleet departs, the carrier offboarding's drivers
paragraph already told him so). **This letter fires only when HE resigns his own
membership.** Operational side only — his capital has the general member redemption
letter, on its own schedule. The employment wall (00 Art. II) holds to the last
word: if the why lives on the employment side, we never ask. Never delivered as a
conversation while he's rolling. Series rules; no winback.*

---

**Subject:** Your departure, filed properly — your record, your legs, and what stays yours
*(alt: "Closing your membership file — everything portable, nothing asked")*

Dear **[first name]**,

With your resignation recorded, this letter closes the operational side properly —
your capital account has its own letter, on its own schedule; nothing here is about
money.

**The why, and the wall:** if your reasons live inside your employment — pay, hours,
a new fleet, the cab — **the same constitution that kept us out of them while you
drove keeps us out of them now.** No exit interview probes across that wall. If your
reasons are about US — the network's conduct, a yard, a dispatch decision — they
belong in the misses section below, in your words, with our answer.

**Your export — your seat-time, in evidence form:** attached is your complete
record: every relay leg with its signed custody handoffs, **your bed-count history
leg by leg** (the number the whole design was drawn against, now yours to carry),
your patronage events with the rules that credited them, your qualification
documents, and the declarations needed to reproduce your own balances from scratch.
**It verifies offline, forever.** Any operation you drive for next can check what
your legs here actually were — signed, not self-reported.

**The unwind, item by item:**

- **Your envelope is revoked as of your departure** — atomically, on the record;
  the timestamp is yours to point at.
- **Assigned legs completed or reassigned cleanly** — **[the list, or "none were
  open."]**
- **[Mid-buy-down drivers only: "Your lease-to-own instrument is its own signed
  deal, and membership was never one of its terms — what happens to it now is
  written in the document you read before signing, not decided in this letter.
  [The instrument's actual departure provision, verbatim.] Nothing about it gets
  improvised at your exit."]**
- **Your streams are sealed, not shredded** — your legs are permanent bilateral
  history, visible to you and each event's parties, never pooled, never anyone's
  market intelligence, exactly as while you drove.

**Said clearly, because it's the sentence this letter exists for:** your membership
was yours alone — **you're not leaving because any fleet left, and no fleet leaves
because you do.** Sovereignty was per-person on your first day and it's per-person
today.

**What survives:** your standing (you leave in good standing, on the record)
**[bad-standing variant: stated factually; the export and every right here —
identical]** · your record, sealed and yours · **[graduated owner-operators: "and
the truck, obviously — the title transferred at completion and no membership event
reaches it."]** · the way back (rejoining is a fresh membership through the
ordinary gate; your bed count is on the record recommending you).

**What didn't go right across your membership, from us first:**

- **[The honest aggregate — the nights out that were ours to explain and whether
  the explanations held, the yard that treated him poorly and its consequence,
  the dispatch friction. If he's departing over a network grievance: it appears
  here, in his terms, with our answer — never omitted because it's awkward.]**

Thank you for the legs, **[first name]** — the relay's whole argument was that a
driver could run freight and still count beds at home, and your count is part of
the proof either way.

**[FREIGHT CO-OP GOVERNANCE — names]**
**[contact]**

## Usage notes (internal — do not send)

- **Scope check before drafting**: confirm this is an actual membership
  resignation on the log. Stopped legs, changed employers, or a departing fleet
  are NOT triggers — sending this letter to a driver who merely went quiet
  converts the voluntary network into one that notices, which is surveillance
  with stationery.
- **The employment wall at exit, absolute**: no probing across it, no
  sympathizing across it, no "sorry to hear about the pay" — the trial letter's
  rule, hardest at the goodbye. Network-conduct grievances go in the misses
  section with answers; employment reasons get the wall sentence and warmth.
- **The export self-verified before delivery** — legs, custody signatures,
  bed-count history, patronage with rule ids, quals, reproduction declarations.
  The bed count is the one number he already knows; getting it wrong in his own
  export is worse than sending nothing.
- **Envelope revocation confirmed on the log before drafting** — atomic,
  timestamped.
- **Mid-buy-down handling**: the instrument's own departure provision, quoted
  verbatim, never paraphrased — counsel wrote it for exactly this moment
  (Tier 3.3). Membership ⊥ instrument; the letter states the separation and
  points at the document.
- **Never-while-rolling holds for the goodbye**: the letter lands whenever; the
  conversation waits for parked — same as every driver contact before it.
- **Two letters, never merged** — operations here, capital in the general member
  redemption letter.
- **Bad-standing / grievance / no-winback** — the family's rules, unchanged: the
  door line is the last word on returning, and nobody calls him about it.
