# Dock go-live checklist — the first window booking

*Fourth of the first-night ceremonies (load, leg, trailer, now the window). The
dock's night one has a reversed polarity the others don't: **the booked window is
an exam WE sit** — her facility declared a window; our freight either arrives
inside it or we open the misses list with our own name. The dock documents' two
disciplines govern throughout: **champion and signer both served** (she runs the
walk-through; nothing operational rests on someone who can't sign for the
building), and **measurement follows freight, symmetrically, from event one** —
she was promised she could see her own basis, and the promise starts existing
tonight. Inherited absolutes: real freight, no showcase; her declared notice and
schedule honored exactly.*

---

## 0 — Preconditions

- [ ] The listing live on the log: the windows as the SIGNER authorized and the
      declared schedule (payment terms, booking lead time, facility rules) as
      the facility stated them — read back before booking anything
- [ ] Champion and signer both know tonight is night one — she gets the
      conversation, the signer gets the confirmation in writing; neither
      learns about the first booking from the dock floor
- [ ] The booking rail exercised on the test log (book → confirm → arrival →
      completion, the event chain) — rehearsed before real freight rides it
- [ ] Accessorial evidence machinery verified BOTH directions: our detention
      lines carry evidence hashes or don't invoice; her facility's touches
      get the same signed pattern — symmetry is the deal, and it must be
      mechanically true before the first event needs it

## 1 — The booking (inside her declared machinery)

- [ ] Real freight, real schedule — no showcase booking
- [ ] The booking placed with HER declared lead time honored — the "booking
      notice that lagged" is the known failure mode of this relationship
      (it made the offboarding letter's misses list in the drafting room);
      night one doesn't get to preview it
- [ ] The window confirmed through the rail, signed — the confirmation is the
      contract, and it's on the record before the truck rolls
- [ ] The driver briefed on the facility's rules: check-in protocol, where to
      stage if early, dock etiquette — her building, her manners, the
      driver's conduct OUR accountability

## 2 — The arrival (our exam)

- [ ] **Arrive inside the window.** The whole design compresses to this line
      tonight — the on-time-against-booking count every trial letter and
      renewal conversation will quote starts 1-for-1, or the miss is named
      to her before she names it
- [ ] Early ≠ virtuous: an early trailer does NOT squat on her apron — staged
      per her rules or off-site until the window opens (the apron squat is
      the other known failure mode; both are inherited misses this checklist
      exists to dodge)
- [ ] The arrival event signed against the booking; every touch at the door
      per the one-pattern — the window's paper trail complete from the first
      minute

## 3 — The turn (evidence both directions, live)

- [ ] Detention clock only per the declared free-time terms, from signed
      arrive/depart events — a detention line with no evidence hash doesn't
      invoice, tonight or ever
- [ ] Her side symmetric: any facility delay/exception attested the same way —
      the both-directions promise demonstrated, not just documented
- [ ] Completion signed; the window closed on the record

## 4 — The measurement honesty (the promise from the walkthrough, kept on day one)

- [ ] Her view of her own basis WORKS tonight: the facility can see what the
      record holds about its own events — before any aggregate exists to
      wonder about
- [ ] k-floors intact: no cross-facility comparison exists at n=1 and none
      gets improvised — "how do we compare" gets the honest answer: no
      comparison until the contributor floor is met, by construction
- [ ] The am-I-being-measured answer restated if asked, verbatim from the
      trial letter's spine: measurement follows freight, symmetric basis,
      her view of her own numbers always

## 5 — The payment and the paper

- [ ] The window settled per the DECLARED schedule — on time, externally,
      attested; slips announced before noticed (live from payment one, same
      as the yard)
- [ ] The signer's record copy: booking, arrival, completion, settlement —
      the file the building keeps; she gets the conversation, the signer
      gets the paper (the dock split, applied to night one's paperwork)

## 6 — Same-day after

- [ ] Her debrief at the dock's pace: did the booking notice work, did the
      driver behave, did the window hold — her answers lead the misses list
- [ ] Misses-first, window-one edition, written tonight — OUR arrival number
      first, whatever it was
- [ ] The weekly status dock block opens: windows booked 1, arrivals in-window
      ____/1, detention events ____ (with evidence), payment on-time 1/1
- [ ] The dock trial letter's clock starts (`dock_trial_followup.md` owns the
      weeks)
- [ ] Escalations to the R-queue, assigned not admired

## 7 — What does NOT happen tonight (refusals, verified)

- [ ] No booking outside her declared notice, no "can you squeeze us in" — the
      lead time was the deal
- [ ] No apron squatting by early equipment — staged per her rules or not there
- [ ] No detention line without its evidence hash — even (especially) if the
      delay was real and the evidence capture failed; the miss is ours then,
      not billable
- [ ] No operational agreement made with anyone who can't sign for the
      building — enthusiasm at the dock floor routes to the signer, always
- [ ] No exclusivity implied: consigned member freight keeps arriving as
      ordinary traffic; the listing was optional capacity, and one booking
      changes neither direction of that
- [ ] No announcement beyond the folds: one window, one arrival (in or out),
      evidence intact, payment on time — counts, not adjectives
