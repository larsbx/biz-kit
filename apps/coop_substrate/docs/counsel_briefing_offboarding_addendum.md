# Counsel briefing — addendum: the offboarding letter set

```
From:  [FOUNDER]
To:    [COUNSEL]
Re:    Addendum to the triaged memo — exit/offboarding templates, what they promise
       in your name, and when each needs you
Date:  [____]
```

**How to read this.** Since the main memo, we drafted the complete exit-letter
family: ten offboarding templates plus companion redemption letters
(`docs/outreach/`, mapped in `offboarding_index.md`). They are templates for events
that have not happened, so **nothing here changes the main memo's triage** — each
item below attaches to an existing tier and comes due when that tier's program
wakes. We list them now because several letters make commitments *in your name*
("per counsel's process," "counsel-reviewed shape"), and you should know what
you've been volunteered for before the first exit lands.

The same discipline applies: every gated letter is a draft until its gate is true,
your open questions default everything to closed, and the letters themselves
instruct the sender that a template is "a starting point for review, never a
bypass."

---

## A.1 — Holder communications at exit [extends 3.2, SECURITIES]

Two templates are holder communication under whatever exemption you build: the
**redemption cycle report** (all holders, each cycle) and the **property
offboarding letter** (an individual holder redeemed to zero). Both templates
hard-code per-cycle / per-instance counsel review, counsel-inserted language
blocks, and NAV-presentation-per-counsel. Estate- and incapacity-driven share
wind-downs are carved out of the template entirely — your process, start to
finish. *Deliverable when 3.2 engages:* review both templates as part of the
exemption strategy; define distribution and delivery-record requirements; the
estate/incapacity procedure. *Plugs in as:* the templates' bracketed counsel
blocks; delivery-record practice.

## A.2 — Worker separation paperwork [extends 3.4, LEGAL]

The worker offboarding letter promises "final documents per the chartered
separation terms — the same counsel-reviewed shape you read before you signed,"
i.e., separation and final tax paperwork whose form was defined at onboarding,
not improvised at exit. *Deliverable when 3.4 engages:* the separation-document
shape for the service co-op's chartered terms (and any facility staffing),
including final tax documents. *Plugs in as:* the worker co-op's chartered
documents; the letter's paperwork paragraph.

## A.3 — The employment wall, at exit [extends 3.4]

The driver letters enforce a strict wall: the freight co-op never employs
drivers, never discusses pay/hours, and its exit letter conducts no probing
across that wall. We believe this discipline *supports* the non-employer posture.
*Deliverable when 3.4 engages:* confirm the letters' language creates no implied
employment relationship or duty, and flag any sentence you'd cut. *Plugs in as:*
template edits.

## A.4 — Instrument departure clauses, verbatim [extends 3.3, LEGAL]

The driver offboarding letter handles a mid-buy-down departure by quoting the
lease-to-own instrument's own departure provision **verbatim, never
paraphrased** — which assumes each state's cleared instrument HAS an explicit
membership-departure provision. *Deliverable when 3.3 engages:* ensure each
state's instrument contains one; its text becomes the letter's bracket.
*Plugs in as:* the instrument documents themselves.

## A.5 — Estate routing of redemption schedules [extends 3.3/3.4-adjacent; new]

The member redemption letter states plainly: "if you die mid-schedule, the
balance routes to your estate — machinery, not goodwill." *Deliverable when the
first redemption schedule opens:* the probate/beneficiary mechanics per our
states (beneficiary designation at onboarding vs. estate process), and the tax
shape of patronage redemptions generally (1099-PATR or otherwise).
*Plugs in as:* membership-document beneficiary language; the schedule's payout
paperwork.

## A.6 — Records at exit: sealed, not shredded, extended to members [extends 1.3]

The main memo's 1.3 covers interviewees. The exit letters extend the same
posture to members and listings: on departure the member receives a complete
verifiable export of their own records, while the ledger's records remain —
append-only, sealed, bilateral-visibility, never pooled or published.
*Deliverable (with 1.3 or on first member exit):* confirm the same
sealed-not-shredded posture is clean for member/listing business records (B2B,
owners not consumers) in our states. *Plugs in as:* nothing mechanical — the
letters already say exactly this; we need it to stay true.

## A.7 — Chapter dissolution mechanics [new; dormant until a second chapter exists]

The chapter offboarding letter executes the inter-chapter agreement's exit
clause (in-flight completion, netting to zero, access termination) and commits
the surviving chapter to retaining the dissolved chapter's checkpoint files
indefinitely as tamper-evidence for its former members. The dissolving chapter's
internal wind-down is its own counsel's, entirely. *Deliverable when a second
chapter approaches reality (with the agreement template's review):* confirm the
retention commitment and the letter create no duties or liabilities toward the
dissolved chapter's members, and review the agreement's exit article.
*Plugs in as:* agreement template edits.

## A.8 — The standing silences (flag only if they worry you)

Two patterns recur deliberately across all ten letters; we believe both are
low-risk and want to know if you disagree: **(a)** written no-recontact
commitments ("the door does not ring," removal from all future contact
expectations) — kept operationally; **(b)** near shares, an absolute no-re-buy
rule — no valuation talk, no "window" language, in any exit document.
*Deliverable:* none unless either creates a problem we don't see.
