# Counsel briefing — cover email (sending the memo + addendum)

*The email that puts the triaged memo and its offboarding addendum on counsel's
desk. Founder-voiced, short, and honest about the only thing that's actually
urgent. Send with the attachments listed at the memo's and addendum's feet; the
bracketed opener adapts to whether this is a new engagement or an existing
relationship.*

---

**Subject:** Freight cooperative — legal questions, triaged; one tier is urgent, the rest is context

Dear **[name]**,

**[New engagement: "Thank you for taking this on — here is the promised written
picture." / Existing: "As discussed, here is the full written picture."]**

I'm building a member-owned freight cooperative whose rules are enforced by
software — prohibited acts aren't policed after the fact; the system simply can't
record them until a clearance from you exists as a signed configuration event.
Practically, that means your answers become switches, your open questions leave
every door closed by default, and nothing I've built can outrun your review.

Two documents attached:

1. **The main memo** — every legal question the project generates, triaged by
   when it blocks. **Only Tier 1 is time-sensitive**: it gates recorded
   interviews with a handful of carrier owners that I'd like to start this
   month — recording-consent posture for the listed states, honorarium
   treatment, and a review of the consent structure. Tiers 2 and 3 are context
   so nothing later surprises you; several Tier 3 programs are deliberately
   dormant and wake only on written triggers.

2. **The offboarding addendum** — since the memo, I drafted the complete set of
   exit letters (member departures, share redemptions, worker separations, and
   the like). Several make commitments in your name — "per counsel's process,"
   "counsel-reviewed shape" — so the addendum lists what you've been volunteered
   for and ties each item to the memo tier it rides with. Nothing in it is
   urgent, and no letter gated on your review can send without it.

**The ask this week:** a scope-and-fee conversation, then Tier 1 top-down. If it's
easier to start from questions than documents, the three Tier 1 deliverables are
in the memo's first page and I can walk you through them in twenty minutes.

One habit of this project you'll notice throughout: where a question is
genuinely open, the system's answer is "not yet" — so please never feel pressure
to answer quickly. An unanswered item costs us patience, never compliance.

Thank you,

**[FOUNDER — name]**
**[phone · email]**

*Attachments: counsel_briefing.md (as PDF) · counsel_briefing_offboarding_addendum.md

## Usage notes (internal — do not send)

- **Fill the states list in memo 1.1 BEFORE sending** — the recording-consent
  question is unanswerable without it, and it's the one deliverable on the clock.
- **PDF the two memos** — counsel gets documents, not a git tutorial; repo access
  is offered, never required.
- **The twenty-minute walk-through offer is real** — have the three Tier 1
  deliverables ready as spoken questions.
- **No deadline pressure in any follow-up** — the closes-by-default posture is
  the relationship's foundation; a rushed counsel is a hedged counsel.
- **Track the engagement's outputs as the memo says**: each clearance arrives as
  a declared constant with counsel's reference in the note — the reply email is
  not the record; the log is.

09 §1, the memos' own framing · clearance-as-declared-constant →
honorarium_rail.md pattern (Tier 1.2), the memo's "plugs in as" discipline.*
