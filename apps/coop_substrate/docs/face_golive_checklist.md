# Face go-live checklist — the first routed load

*The brokerage's opening night, run like everything else here: gates verified from
the log, then one load treated as a ceremony — because the first routed load is
the co-op keeping, in one motion, promises made across half the document set: the
hub's "disclosed margin from load one," session two's "ratio declared before the
Face routes," the shipper page's "evidence-bound from tender to invoice," and the
constitution's "voluntary, load by load, forever." **A first load that cuts one
corner teaches every member which promises were decorative.** The checklist's
altitude: one load, done exactly right, beats a launch week.*

---

## 0 — The legal gates (none of these are ours to wave)

- [ ] Broker authority GRANTED (not just filed): the 3.5 packet's evidence gate
      passed, the grant in hand, the bond (BMC-84 or counsel's chosen form)
      active — counsel confirms the operating posture in writing
- [ ] Counsel's Face items (the shipper page's precondition) cleared and
      recorded as declared constants with counsel references — whatever
      remains uncleared stays structurally off, verified not remembered
- [ ] Insurance posture per counsel for brokered freight confirmed — the
      carrier's own coverage verified through the activation chain (3.6's
      verification discipline), never assumed

## 1 — The charter gates (declared before first evaluation, provably)

- [ ] **The I1 split ratio: DECLARED ON THE LOG** — session two's diarized
      trigger fires here, as a blocking precondition: no ratio event, no first
      load. Read the constant back from the log, don't trust the meeting memory
- [ ] The Face's session-three constants declared: pricing corridors, the
      routed_freight weight activated (the name session two reserved, now
      carrying a weight — margin that lands before the weight exists cannot
      accrue, and "we'll credit it retroactively" is retro-fit, which is void)
- [ ] Operator compensation re-verified against the appointment: **fee or
      salary, never a spread** — the Face is where that rule meets its first
      temptation, so it gets re-read the week the Face goes live
- [ ] The margin-disclosure fold LIVE: a member who routes can see the spread
      on their own load, from their own view, same day — demo it on the test
      log first

## 2 — The participation gates (voluntary, mechanically)

- [ ] Every carrier who might take the tender has a SIGNED envelope on the log
      (H5 — their signature, their ceiling, their policy); no envelope, no
      tender to them, no exceptions for enthusiasm
- [ ] Bypass stated once more to the participating carriers, in words: routing
      through the Face is per-load, their choice, and taking this load creates
      no duty to take the next — the ad's "nobody owns your yes," now
      operational
- [ ] The shipper side per its own documents: the first lane's shipper onboarded
      per the scheduling email's manners, expectations set at the one-pager's
      register — zero volume promises in either direction
- [ ] Dispatch acceptance green: the 4A rail's out-of-envelope-never-acts
      invariant re-run against the ACTUAL envelopes signed above

## 3 — The load (one ceremony, every link)

- [ ] The tender: signed, carrying its terms, routed to carriers whose
      envelopes admit it — acceptance is the carrier's signed act, in-envelope,
      HOS-feasible per the accept check
- [ ] Custody: every handoff condition-attested per the one pattern — the first
      brokered load's chain must be COMPLETE, because it will be the example
- [ ] Delivery → invoice: byte-reproducible, detention/accessorial lines
      carrying evidence hashes or absent — rendered artifacts, human-reviewed,
      sent per whatever 2.1–2.3 clearances exist (uncleared = manual, still)
- [ ] Settlement: EXTERNAL, attested on the rail — money never moved
      on-platform before the Face and it doesn't start tonight
- [ ] **The margin, disclosed same-day**: the spread on this load visible to
      the routing member in their own view — load one, as promised, not load
      "once the dashboard is ready"
- [ ] The split applied AS DECLARED: captured margin divided
      patronage-vs-retained per the I1 ratio from §1, the patronage crediting
      under `routed_freight`'s declared weight, the rule id on the credit

## 4 — Same-day after

- [ ] The routing member walks their own load's whole story in their view:
      tender, acceptance, custody, invoice, margin, credit — and is ASKED what
      didn't match what they were promised (their answer opens the misses list)
- [ ] Misses-first, load-one edition, written tonight: the link that needed a
      manual nudge, the disclosure that lagged an hour, the parse that needed
      review — this list is the go-live report's spine
- [ ] The governance aggregate: one load, gates held, split applied, margin
      disclosed — counts and event references, no adjectives
- [ ] Escalations check: anything the load raised sits in the R-queue
      (`mix cockpit.queue`), assigned, not admired
- [ ] The follow-up clocks start: the shipper trial letter's and carrier Face
      letter's own schedules own the reckonings — this checklist doesn't
      duplicate them, it starts their clocks

## 5 — What does NOT happen at go-live (refusals, verified)

- [ ] No volume commitments made to anyone to "make launch work" — the Face
      routes what members choose to route, including approximately nothing at
      first
- [ ] No margin taken off-book to smooth the ratio's optics — the fold IS the
      book; there is nowhere else, and the emptiness of anywhere-else is
      checkable
- [ ] No announcement outruns the record: whatever is said about load one
      resolves to folds anyone can recompute — the zero-numbers discipline
      applied to our own milestone
- [ ] No second load scheduled until load one's misses list exists — velocity
      is a decision for the day after the reckoning, not the day of the launch
