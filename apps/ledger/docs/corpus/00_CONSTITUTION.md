# 00_CONSTITUTION.md — Constitutional Principles

Register: jurisprudential (uṣūl al-fiqh terms native, English gloss at first use). Concern: the constraint set C that all other documents implement. Supremacy: this document constrains all others; conflicts resolve upward to it.

---

## Article I — Interpretation

1. **Current-state supremacy.** The most recent settled decision governs. Conflicts are flagged, never silently reconciled; refinements must preserve the superseded rule's purpose (e.g., carrier-*owned* → carrier-*operated*).
2. **Adjudication (tarjīḥ).** Contested questions resolve through the ruling interface (09); pending a ruling, contested instruments are gated N (not-yet).

## Article II — Sovereignty

1. Carrier sovereignty over drivers is absolute. No crossover between carriers, nor into any co-op workforce; symmetric for every worker co-op.
2. Worker income is sovereign; shared infrastructure is collectively owned. Pool assets, never earnings.
3. Relationship edges (customers, contacts, lanes) are sovereign member data. Disclosure is voluntary, signed, never incentive-pressured beyond ordinary patronage.
4. The co-op never holds operational control of a member's equipment, never dispatches power units, never employs drivers. Motor-carrier operational control by the federation or any chapter organ is forbidden (ḥarām).
5. No stage of the network is mandatory for members. Offering, not enclosure: bypass is permitted (mubāḥ).

## Article III — Financial constraints

1. All mechanics are risk-sharing, real-asset-backed, or fee-for-service. No interest (ribā), no speculative instruments, no material term-uncertainty (gharar) in contracts.
2. Return without corresponding risk is forbidden; risk sits where margin sits (vacancy risk justifies lease spread; capital loss falls on capital in muḍāraba).
3. All material terms are pre-declared, versioned, signed. Improvised terms under stress are forbidden.
4. Legal stratagems (ḥiyal) reconstructing forbidden structures are forbidden regardless of formal validity; the platform enforces this structurally (08: invalid ⇒ unrepresentable).
5. Conventional insurance and similar necessities are permitted as bounded necessity (ḍarūra/ḥāja): genuine need, minimized extent, demonstrable exit path.
6. Idle capital follows the treasury policy (05); credited interest is purified to charity, never revenue.

## Article IV — Governance

1. One member, one vote — always. Economic participation scales with shares; governance never does.
2. Concentration limits: per-member share ceiling κ; aggregate fixed-liability ceiling L_max; all such constants are charter events declared before first evaluation.
3. Capital follows evidence: no tier of capital commitment without its declared evidence gate (03).
4. Captured intermediary rent resolves to member-visible flows via declared splits; relocation of rent to any co-op organ's own account is forbidden.
5. The federation is a union of sovereign locality chapters; inter-chapter defaults are non-interference, bilateral agreement, and netting settlement.

## Article V — The deontic lattice

Five tiers govern every operation; the platform (08) maps them to enforcement:

| Tier | Term | Enforcement |
|---|---|---|
| Obligatory | wājib | failure is a violation event; guards watch it |
| Recommended | mandūb | defaults and reminders |
| Permitted | mubāḥ | unrestricted |
| Discouraged | makrūh | permitted, logged, provenance-annotated, pattern-audited |
| Forbidden | ḥarām | unrepresentable where structural; block-tier where behavioral |

Time-decay is legitimate: a permission may be chartered with a decay schedule (e.g., incubator authority mandūb → makrūh past its sunset), enforced by a hard sunset event.

## Article VI — Protected objectives (maqāṣid)

A top class of operations is forbidden to all capabilities, human or agent, without exception or delegation: falsifying provenance, altering append-only history, operating uncovered equipment with live freight, coercing disclosure of sovereign edges, and any act extinguishing member sovereignty defined in Article II.

## Article VII — Amendment

Amendments are signed governance events, chapter-ratified per charter, and must state which prior article or settled decision they supersede. Silent drift is void.
