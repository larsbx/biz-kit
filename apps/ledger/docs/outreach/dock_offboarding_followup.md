# Dock-manager offboarding follow-up — template (when a facility delists)

*The narrowest exit in the set, and the letter must say so: **what ends is the
listing; what continues is everything else** — member freight still delivers to her
doors as ordinary consigned traffic, still evidence-bound, and her facility stays
measured where freight flows, because measurement was never about the listing.
Serves champion and signer both (her the walk-through, the signer the file), same as
every dock document. Cause variants: the facility's choice (via the signer — zero
probing), the corridor's (explained as design), or disuse (mutual, factual). Series
rules; no winback.*

---

**Subject:** Closing the window listings — what ends, what doesn't, and your record
*(alt: "The delisting, filed properly — and the part that continues unchanged")*

Hi **[first name]**,

**[The cause, plainly — pick one:]**
**[Facility's choice: "With [signer]'s decision to delist, this letter files it
properly — no questions about the why; listing was always optional and so was
staying listed."]**
**[Corridor: "The [corridor]'s staging wound down, and booked windows went with it —
the gates that opened the flow closed it automatically; nothing about your building
changed."]**
**[Disuse: "The windows haven't booked in [period] — rather than let a dormant
listing imply something neither of us means, we're filing it closed; relisting is
one message whenever demand and your doors line up again."]**

**The line this letter exists to draw:** the listing ends; **the relationship
doesn't.** Member freight consigned to your facility keeps arriving exactly as
before — every touch signed, every accessorial line carrying its evidence or not
invoicing at all, in both directions, same as always. Your building was a
counterparty to this network before it listed a single window, and it stays one
after the last listing comes down.

**And the measurement honesty, restated at the exit:** your facility stays measured
**where member freight flows** — same contributor floors, same symmetric basis,
delisted or not. Measurement follows freight, not listings; that was true at the
walkthrough and it's true today, and your view of your own basis doesn't close.

**The final accounting:** **[window payments — "all [N] booked windows settled at
your declared schedule; nothing open." Open items block the letter.]**

**Your record:** attached is the complete history of the listing period — every
booked window, every arrival against it (ours to be graded on, one last time:
**[the aggregate — on-time rate across the listing's life]**), and every accessorial
event with its evidence. Yours and [signer]'s to keep; the file copy goes to
[signer] with this letter.

**The gate back:** relisting is one message from whoever signs for the building —
your read on that stays as valuable as it was the first time. The declared schedule
would be yours to set fresh; nothing carries over that you don't want carried.

**What didn't go right across the listing, from us first:**

- **[The honest aggregate — the early trailer that sat on the apron anyway, the
  booking notice that lagged, the misses from the trial letter that stayed fixed
  or didn't. If the disuse variant: the demand shortfall in plain numbers — the
  windows we didn't fill were ours to fill.]**

Thanks for listing the doors, **[first name]** — and see you at the dock either way,
which is rather the point.

**[FOUNDER / FACE OPERATOR — name]**
**[contact]**

## Usage notes (internal — do not send)

- **The ends-vs-continues line is the letter's spine** — draw it in the first
  paragraph after the cause, and make sure operations actually match it: her
  facility's counterparty treatment (evidence-bound accessorials, symmetric
  measurement access) must be verifiably identical post-delisting, or the letter
  lied.
- **The measurement honesty is mandatory**: a manager who delists and assumes the
  scoring stopped will feel ambushed by the next measured event — say
  freight-not-listings here, where it's kind, rather than later, where it's a
  dispute.
- **Champion and signer both served**, same as every dock document — she gets the
  letter and the conversation; the signer gets the record copy, named in the
  letter.
- **Settle-before-send**; the record export self-checked (booked windows, arrivals,
  accessorial evidence — the listing period's complete file).
- **Our arrival aggregate appears in HER record section** — graded one last time,
  the our-scorecard-first doctrine applied to the goodbye.
- **Disuse variant owns the shortfall**: "the windows we didn't fill were ours to
  fill" — demand was always our side of the listing; never let disuse read as her
  doors' failure.
- **No winback machinery** — the relist door is stated once; the continuing
  operational relationship makes any drip campaign both unnecessary and corrosive
  (you'll see her at the dock; that's the channel, and it's not a channel).
- **The war-story warmth survives**: the champion relationship was never the
  listing's — coffee stays coffee, the consent boundary stays the boundary.
