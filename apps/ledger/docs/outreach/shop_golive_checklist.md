# Shop go-live checklist — the first referral

*Fifth and last of the first-night ceremonies (load, leg, trailer, window, now
the referral) — and the strangest, because its centerpiece is an absence: **the
introduction is the entire product, and it's given away.** The moment the
referral connects, the member and the shop own the relationship — the invoice is
his, the price is his, the future calls are theirs, and the only artifact the
network keeps is the signed completion that builds his track record. The known
failure mode is the misroute (a job outside his declared capabilities), and the
known temptation is the tail (a fee, a cut, a "network rate," a commission on
call two) — night one exists to dodge the first and refuse the second in front
of witnesses.*

---

## 0 — Preconditions

- [ ] The listing live: his capabilities declared IN HIS WORDS on the log —
      read them back; the match below runs against what he said, not what we
      remember
- [ ] **The money-absence verified structurally**: no mechanism exists by which
      the referral, the job, or the invoice touches us — re-check the trial
      letter's spine BEFORE the first event, because "we never touched a
      dollar" must be true from dollar zero
- [ ] The referral rail rehearsed on the test log: need → match → referral →
      acceptance → completion, the event chain green
- [ ] Membership ⊥ listing confirmed in his file: whichever he holds, tonight
      exercises only the listing — nothing about the referral requires or
      implies the other

## 1 — The referral (matched, offered, his to decline)

- [ ] Real breakdown, real member — no staged job to christen the flow
- [ ] **The match runs against his DECLARED capabilities** — the misroute is
      this relationship's known failure mode (it led the trial letter's misses
      in the drafting room); a job his declaration doesn't cover doesn't route
      to him, however close his bays are
- [ ] Bays-first manners: the referral arrives as an offer respecting his
      current load — he can decline, declining costs him nothing (no standing
      penalty, no quiet deprioritization; the next match runs the same), and
      that's said in words on referral one
- [ ] The member's side is a choice too: the referral is an introduction,
      never an assignment — where he goes is his call

## 2 — The connection (and the letting-go)

- [ ] The introduction made per the flow: the member gets the shop's card, the
      shop gets the heads-up — both signed onto the record as the referral
      event
- [ ] **From this moment the relationship is theirs**: scheduling, scope,
      price, invoice — all between shop and member, direct. We are not cc'd,
      not consulted, not in the loop, and the checklist's job is to verify
      that nobody from the network inserts themselves "just to make sure it
      goes well"
- [ ] His price is his: no network rate suggested, no anchor floated, no
      member told "what it should cost" — never-anchor-a-rate, live from
      referral one

## 3 — The completion (the one artifact we keep)

- [ ] The dual-signed job completion when the work's done — shop and member,
      outcome recorded factually — the single network-side artifact of the
      whole job, and the first brick of his track record
- [ ] Graded by outcomes, never by stars: no rating prompt exists, none gets
      improvised for launch — the completion says what happened; adjectives
      stay out of the record
- [ ] Chains verified same-day; the completion where it belongs, signed by
      whom it claims

## 4 — The empty ledger (the promise, checked on day one)

- [ ] After the job settles between them: **our accounting of it is EMPTY, and
      someone looks** — the offboarding letter will one day claim "we never
      touched a dollar, verified"; that verification practice starts with
      dollar zero, not at the exit
- [ ] Any group-purchasing balance he holds stays membership-side, untouched
      and unmentioned in the referral's paperwork — the unbundling, kept in
      the files too

## 5 — Same-day (and next-day) after

- [ ] His debrief, at the counter, at his pace: was the job inside his
      declaration? Did the member show on time? Would he take referral two? —
      his answers lead the misses list
- [ ] The member's debrief separately: did the shop deliver? — the outcome
      grading's input, bilateral, never shared across the two of them beyond
      the signed completion both already hold
- [ ] Misses-first, referral-one edition, written tonight: the match that
      almost misrouted, the heads-up that lagged, the member who ran late to
      his bay
- [ ] The weekly status shop block opens: referrals 1, accepted ____, completed
      ____, misroutes 0 (or the honest number, leading the list)
- [ ] The shop trial letter's clock starts (`shop_trial_followup.md` owns the
      weeks); escalations to the R-queue, assigned not admired

## 6 — What does NOT happen tonight (refusals, verified)

- [ ] **No fee, no cut, no commission — tonight or ever**: the first referral
      is when someone suggests "even a small platform fee"; the answer is the
      anti-marketplace promise, and it has no small versions
- [ ] No follow-up insertion into their relationship: call two between this
      member and this shop is their edge — never routed, never counted, no
      commission tail, and its invisibility to us is checkable
- [ ] No misroute papered over: if the match was wrong, the referral is
      withdrawn with an apology to BOTH sides and the misroute leads tonight's
      misses — never "he can probably handle it"
- [ ] No acceptance pressure: his declined referral routes onward without
      editorial; the decline is not data anyone acts on
- [ ] No stars, no rankings, no "preferred shop" language — his track record
      is signed history, and night one doesn't get to invent a leaderboard
- [ ] No announcement beyond the folds: one referral, one completion (or one
      honest misroute), zero dollars touched — counts, not adjectives
