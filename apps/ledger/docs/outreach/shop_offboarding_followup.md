# Shop-owner offboarding follow-up — template (when a shop delists)

*A narrow exit like the dock's, and narrower still: when the capability listing comes
down, only the ROUTING ends — every member who already had his number keeps calling
it, because those relationships were never ours. The letter's signature is **the
empty accounting section**: we never touched a dollar of his, so there is nothing to
settle, and the emptiness is the promise keeping itself to the last page. Cause
variants: his choice (zero probing) or disuse (owned as OUR routing shortfall).
Membership, if he holds it, is untouched — unbundled to the end. Series rules; no
winback.*

---

**Subject:** Closing the capability listing — the empty ledger, and everything that doesn't change
*(alt: "The delisting, filed — with nothing to settle, which was the deal")*

Hi **[first name]**,

**[The cause, plainly — pick one:]**
**[His choice: "Your delisting message did what it always would — this letter is the
paperwork keeping up. No questions about the why."]**
**[Disuse: "The routing hasn't sent you work in [period] — rather than let a dormant
listing sit, we're filing it closed. Plainly: the referrals we didn't send were ours
to send; demand in your area was always our side of the listing, and it ran thin.
Your bays did nothing but wait, which is not a shop's failure."]**

**The accounting between us:** **empty — and that was always the point.** Every job
the network ever routed was invoiced by you, to the member, at your price; we never
touched a dollar, so there is nothing to reconcile, refund, or chase. The absence
you're reading is the anti-marketplace promise, kept through the exit.
**[Member with group purchasing live: "Your parts-program enrollment is
membership-side, not listing-side — it continues untouched, spread audit and all."]**

**What ends, and what never was ours to end:** the routing stops today. **The phone
numbers don't** — every member who called you before the listing, or found you
through it and kept your card, keeps calling you directly, and that traffic was
never ours to see, count, or route around. Your customer relationships were
sovereign walking in; they're sovereign walking out; nothing in between changed that.

**Your track record:** the reputation your finished jobs built — signed work, graded
by outcomes, never by stars — **stays what it is: sealed history, yours to point
at.** Nothing new accrues because nothing new routes; nothing old erodes because the
record doesn't work that way.

**[Member paragraph, where he holds membership:]** **"Delisting is not resigning:
your vote, your member rate, and the buying program all continue exactly as before —
the listing and the membership were separate conversations going in, and they're
separate going out."**

**The gate back:** relisting is one message; your capabilities re-declare fresh (your
words, as always — bays change, lists should too).

**What didn't go right across the listing, from us first:**

- **[The honest aggregate — the misroute from the trial letter and whether the fix
  held, the referral messages that lagged, the member no-show and its consequence.
  If the disuse variant: the routing shortfall itself, in the numbers he can see.]**

Thanks for listing the bays, **[first name]** — and since nothing about who calls you
changed, this is less a goodbye than a filing. Coffee stands.

**[FOUNDER — name]**
**[contact]**

## Usage notes (internal — do not send)

- **The empty-accounting paragraph must be LITERALLY true** — verify no mechanism
  ever touched his revenue (the trial letter's spine, re-checked at exit). Any
  group-purchasing balance is membership-side and named separately, never mixed
  into the listing's ledger.
- **The direct-relationships severance is restated and honored operationally**:
  members calling him directly is not tracked, not counted, not followed up on —
  their edges, his phone, nobody's data.
- **Disuse = our routing shortfall, owned in numbers** — the referrals-not-sent
  logic, same family as the yard's non-renewal and the dock's unfilled windows:
  demand was always the network's side.
- **Membership-vs-listing unbundled to the end** — the member paragraph appears
  only for members and says the separation plainly; a non-member gets no membership
  talk at all (and, like the yard's non-member, nothing about data because there's
  nothing to say).
- **Track-record permanence stated exactly**: sealed history, no new accrual, no
  erosion — never imply delisting dims what the signed jobs already said.
- **No winback** — the relist line once; the continuing direct relationships make
  any campaign both pointless and insulting.
- **The warmth survives on its own channel**: coffee was never the listing's —
  same as the dock's rule, said in the letter's last line because this persona
  earned the informality.
- **The diner rule, shop edition**: shops talk to shops at the parts counter; the
  empty ledger is the story that travels.
