# SchoolOps contributor policy

The imported curriculum is a pinned snapshot of `larsbx/school-ops`.
Read `priv/content/AGENTS.md`, `priv/content/docs/curriculum-model.md`
and `priv/content/docs/trivium-quadrivium.md` before editing curriculum.
Do not change source IDs, lesson statuses, assessment criteria or pedagogy
during a mechanical source sync. In particular, music is excluded and
Common Core is not the organizing model. Keep individual learner records
and class assignment URLs out of this public app. Record original blob IDs
and commit for every source sync; do not fork editorial authority.
