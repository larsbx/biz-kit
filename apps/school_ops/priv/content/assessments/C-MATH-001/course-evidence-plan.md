# Later-unit evidence plan

- Assessment ID: A-MATH-001-03
- Course: [C-MATH-001](../../curriculum/mathematics/grade-2/README.md)
- Intended learners: 2nd grade
- Status: draft
- Purpose: plan evidence for outcomes 005–013 within the chosen classical arts
- Tasks, timings, and teacher keys: not yet authored
- Readiness review: pending; draft introduced in merged [PR #1](https://github.com/larsbx/school-ops/pull/1)

This is an authoring backlog, not an assessment that can yet be administered.
All evidence includes accurate names or representations, a reason, and an
explanation to another person. Criteria are local proposals to refine in context.

| Outcome | Planned investigation | Evidence to seek |
| --- | --- | --- |
| O-MATH-001-005 | Compose and separate small quantities with objects and mental methods | Accurate result, a sensible method, and an explanation of the operation |
| O-MATH-001-006 | Interpret an everyday situation and model the calculation | Model matches the story; answer and unit are justified |
| O-MATH-001-007 | Calculate a larger amount by decomposing and regrouping units | Representation and calculation agree; explain why the amount is preserved |
| O-MATH-001-008 | Measure a length with two chosen unit sizes | Correct use of each unit; explain why the numeric counts differ |
| O-MATH-001-009 | Show order and a calculation on a marked line | Consistent spacing, matching movement and result, clear explanation |
| O-MATH-001-010 | Keep dated observations of an accessible sky feature and compare intervals | Distinguish actual observations, missing observations, and calendar labels; reason about a possible cycle |
| O-MATH-001-011 | Make a table or diagram from counts or observations | Accurate representation; explanation separates recorded data from prediction |
| O-MATH-001-012 | Construct a shape or whole from equal parts | Correct attributes and equal amounts; justify how parts form the whole |
| O-MATH-001-013 | Pair, group, share, double, or halve a collection | Correct relationship, explicit unit, and a reason showing preservation or change |

Before a unit is ready, author tasks and keys, identify the primary art, verify
resources, set task-specific criteria, and record support, materials, and time.
For astronomy, distinguish a physical cycle from human timekeeping conventions.
Individual observations and results belong in private records.
