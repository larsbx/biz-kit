# 0004 — Modern usage within classical progression

- Decision date: 2026-10-09 (America/Chicago)
- Status: accepted design direction
- Framework: [decision 0003](0003-trivium-quadrivium.md)

## Decision

The user clarified that modern usage is beneficial while the underlying structure
and progression are built on classical foundations.

Use contemporary notation, examples, manipulatives, measuring tools, diagrams,
audio, observation aids, and online practice when they advance understanding.
Choose these applications from the course's goals and prerequisite relationships.

The Trivium develops accurate language, sound reasoning, and clear expression.
The mathematical progression begins with units and numerical relationships,
then connects these to geometry and astronomy. Modern resource catalogues
supply materials; they do not prescribe that progression.

## Application

Keep the first course in modern place-value notation while investigating units,
composition, invariants, and explanations. Include practical situations throughout.
Develop the later arts through real geometric and astronomical work,
with appropriate modern resources reviewed as those tasks are authored.
