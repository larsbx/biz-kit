# 0005 — Exclude music

- Decision date: 2026-10-09 (America/Chicago)
- Status: accepted scope constraint
- Applies to: course maps, lessons, resources, and assessment

## Decision

The user directed: no music. Omit music study and musical activities from the
curriculum. Retain arithmetic, geometry, and astronomy as the selected mathematical
arts, with grammar, logic, and rhetoric throughout.

Historical descriptions may identify music as a member of the Quadrivium while
stating its exclusion here. Do not add rhythm or harmony work under another label.
The user's approval of modern usage in [decision 0004](0004-modern-usage-classical-progression.md)
continues to apply to the included studies.
