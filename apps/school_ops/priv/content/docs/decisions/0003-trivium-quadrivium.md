# 0003 — Organize the curriculum through the Trivium and Quadrivium

- Decision date: 2026-10-09 (America/Chicago)
- Status: accepted framework decision
- Applies to: curriculum structure, course plans, lessons, resources, and assessment
- First course: [second-grade mathematics](../../curriculum/mathematics/grade-2/README.md)

## Decision

The user directed that the Trivium and Quadrivium organize the curriculum.
Use grammar, logic, and rhetoric as active disciplines in learning, and organize
mathematical studies through arithmetic, geometry, music, and astronomy.

The later exclusion in [decision 0005](0005-no-music.md) governs the active scope:
arithmetic, geometry, and astronomy remain; music is omitted.

The numerical ranges and lesson durations are adjustable teaching proposals.
Second grade identifies the starting audience; prerequisites come from entry work.
Apply the framework in tasks and explanations as defined in
[the teaching model](../trivium-quadrivium.md).

## Consequences

- Course maps identify the arts and their relationships.
- Every lesson includes names/notation, reasoning, and communication of understanding.
- Astronomy includes observation and interpretation of sky cycles and motion.
  The historical musical art is excluded from the active curriculum.
- Resource providers supply exercises or materials suited to these goals. Provider
  taxonomies do not determine the curriculum.
