## Decision

Register it as `C-MATH-001` and prioritize its entry check and first teaching unit.

## Implementation proposal

Begin with place value, using an entry check to identify review needs. Develop
the course through arithmetic, geometry, and astronomy, with grammar,
logic, and rhetoric active throughout. The framework is established in
[decision 0003](0003-trivium-quadrivium.md).

The proposed lesson durations and local assessment criteria are authoring choices,
not agreed teaching schedules or externally prescribed thresholds.
Khan Academy practice queries may use the explicitly selected `2nd` grade limit.
No standard search filter has been authorized or selected.

## Open choices

Weekly time, dates, learner starting skills, materials on hand,
and the actual online practice sets will be established during course preparation.
