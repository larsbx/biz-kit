## Foundation and progression

Start with units and numbers, then composition and difference, equal groups and
sharing, magnitude and form, and observed sky cycles.
This progression develops arithmetic relationships before applying them in
geometry and astronomy. Ideas are revisited across the arts.

Modern place-value notation, manipulatives, clocks, graphs, practical problems,
and online exercises help express and investigate these ideas. Choose their use
from the learning goal. Their catalogues do not determine the course sequence.

Use the [entry check](../../../assessments/C-MATH-001/entry-check.md) to identify
review needs, then begin the [first unit](units/place-value/README.md). Music is
excluded by [decision 0005](../../../docs/decisions/0005-no-music.md).
All teaching material is draft. Numerical ranges are adjustable authoring choices;
start with quantities the learner can demonstrate and extend after checking evidence.

## Trivium throughout the course

- Grammar: name the unit, amount, relation, form, or pattern; read and use symbols
  accurately; practice useful facts and definitions.
- Logic: preserve an amount while regrouping, test an operation, compare reasons,
  and investigate a false claim with a counterexample.
- Rhetoric: teach a solution to another person through speech, diagrams, objects,
  or writing, connecting the claim to its mathematical reason.

Each lesson includes all three. Expectations grow from accurate naming and
concrete demonstrations toward connected explanations; reasoning and explanation
begin in the first unit.

Use practical applications and review throughout. The applications unit brings
those connections together; it does not delay modern usage until the end.
Short arithmetic practice may run alongside later units. Each new study rests
on demonstrated earlier relationships, with review added where needed.

## Materials and evidence

Use objects, drawings, and modern notation together. Select technology when it
makes a relationship visible or supplies useful practice. The
[Khan Academy queue](../../../resources/second-grade-math-searches.md) contains
retrieval status for our goals. Seven optional hundreds-route practice sets have
been reviewed; the [route review](../../../docs/reviews/2026-10-11-khan-place-value.md)
records two-digit-only and thousand gaps. Teaching and assessment records remain draft.

First-unit evidence checks accurate naming, a reasoned model, and an explanation.
Use the [prepared taught-scope routes](units/place-value/README.md#choose-the-teaching-route)
and corresponding assessment form; smaller-range evidence leaves larger work pending.
Later units require authored tasks, reviewed sources, and appropriate materials.
Keep individual learner evidence privately and use it to adjust pacing.

The [framework document](../../../docs/trivium-quadrivium.md) records the classical
vocabulary and our age-appropriate adaptation. The user also explicitly welcomed
modern usage in [decision 0004](../../../docs/decisions/0004-modern-usage-classical-progression.md).
