# Curriculum map: Trivium and Quadrivium

The classical arts organize development, with music excluded by user direction.
K–12 describes the learners we intend to
serve. Specific courses and their pace grow from prerequisites and inquiry.
Modern tools and applications support this structure.

## Trivium across the curriculum

| Art | Development focus | First-course application |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Grammar | Language, names, notation, definitions, and facts | Mathematical names and accurate representations |
| Logic or dialectic | Relations, inference, testing claims, and reasons | Comparison, invariants, and explanations of operations |
| Rhetoric | Clear, ordered expression to another person | Spoken, drawn, or written mathematical demonstrations |

These arts are active in the first course and will also inform dedicated language
and reasoning studies when those courses are selected.
