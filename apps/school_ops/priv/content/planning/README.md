# Teaching plans and reviews

Copy the [weekly-plan template](../templates/weekly-plan.md) to
`planning/YYYY-MM-DD-weekly-plan.md`, using the plan's start date. Link to course,
unit, lesson, and resource records so the plan can be checked against the curriculum.

Set the time budget and dates when planning actual teaching. Choose work after its
prerequisites are covered; leave room for review and changes. Class-specific
assignment URLs are kept in private planning.

Keep the plan `draft` until its [weekly-plan readiness requirements](../docs/curriculum-model.md#readiness-by-record-type)
are met: dates, time budget, ordered sessions, prerequisite/access checks, and
links to ready teaching records. Record the readiness review date and supporting
pull request. After-week observations are filled after delivery.

After the week, record curriculum observations: activities that need revision,
time estimates that changed, resource fit, prerequisite gaps, and follow-up issues.
Use these decisions to prepare the next plan. Keep learner names, scores, and
individual accommodations in private records.
