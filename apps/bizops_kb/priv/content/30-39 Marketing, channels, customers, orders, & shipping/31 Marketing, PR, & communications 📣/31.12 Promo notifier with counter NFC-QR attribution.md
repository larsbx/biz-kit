# Promo notifier with counter NFC-QR attribution

## Decision

Offer restaurants a marketing agent that **notifies opted-in customers of the
current promotion** (e.g. a free item) and **attributes the resulting
in-store visits** through an NFC tag / QR sticker at the checkout counter.
The service is paid a **percentage of the bills of attributed visits**.

Implementation: `larsbx/eatsbot`, domain `EatsbotCore.Marketing` plus the
Telegram extension; see `docs/modules/extensions/pages/marketing.adoc`.

This note records the product and commercial model. It does **not** authorize
signing partner agreements, printing stickers, or messaging customers.

## How it works

1. A sticker (NFC tag + printed QR, same URL) is placed at the counter.
2. A customer's first scan opens the bot and subscribes them to the branch.
   This visit is **not** billable.
3. The restaurant publishes a promotion; the service announces it to
   subscribers.
4. A notified customer returns and scans again. The bot shows the offer and a
   6-character code.
5. The cashier sends `/redeem CODE AMOUNT`. The service books
   `commission = round(amount × rate)`.
6. Monthly invoice = sum of commissions for the branch and period.

## Attribution rule (billing basis)

A visit is billable iff the customer checked in within the attribution window
(default 7 days) after a promotion notification, and no earlier visit already
consumed that notification:

`attributed ⇔ notified ≤ check-in ≤ notified + W ∧ (no attributed visit since notified)`

- One announcement credits at most one visit per customer.
- The rate is fixed on the sticker (touchpoint) and snapshotted per visit:
  changing it later never alters past invoices.
- Visits that don't qualify can still be redeemed at 0 % commission.

## Commercial terms to agree per restaurant

- Commission rate (system default 5 %), on the gross bill of attributed visits.
- Attribution window (default 7 days) and redemption window (default 12 h).
- Who funds the free item: the restaurant.
- Invoice cadence (monthly) and dispute window, with the visit ledger as the
  shared record.
- Customer data: Telegram chat ids only; `/stop` opt-out is honoured
  immediately. Confirm the lawful basis for promotional messaging in the
  restaurant's jurisdiction before launch.

## Open risks

- Relies on cashier discipline to enter `/redeem`; unredeemed visits earn
  nothing. Consider a POS integration later.
- Announcement marks customers notified before delivery is confirmed.
- Telegram-only for now; WhatsApp/SMS would be further channels.
