# Hermes assistant quick cards

One card per channel, printed one per page. Everyone gets the Telegram card;
add the WhatsApp or SMS card only for people enrolled on that extra channel.
Fill every blank before printing. Part of
[[24.12 Hermes assistant on-site deployment]].

## Telegram (everyone)

**Your assistant:** in Telegram, search for ________ and tap Start. This is
your private line to your own agent: message it like a colleague. Only your
enrolled account gets answers.

**The hub:** Buzz, at ________. Everything you send, messages and documents,
lands there first and is routed to the right agent and filed. Team threads
and hand-offs live there too.

**Ask it to**

- Find a document: "Where's the latest ________?"
- Summarise: "Summarise ________ in 5 lines"
- Draft: "Draft a reply to this customer saying ________"
- File something: "Where should this invoice go?"
- Your role's use cases: ________

**Never send in chat**

- Anything on the restricted list: ________
- Passwords, bank details, ID numbers, health information
- If in doubt, use the upload link instead. If you send something restricted,
  the assistant refuses it and replies with the upload link.

**Sending documents**

| Kind of document | Use |
| --- | --- |
| Everyday files (open) | Send in chat, or upload link: ________ |
| Internal files | Upload link only: ________ |
| Restricted files | Upload link only: ________ |

Upload links are write-only and feed into Buzz: you can add files, not see
other people's. Restricted files go straight to the vault.

**If it doesn't answer**

Check you're online and wait 5 minutes. Still nothing? Reach your agent
directly in Buzz and tell ________ (admin).

## WhatsApp (extra channel)

**Your assistant:** save ________ as a contact named "Assistant". Message it
like a colleague. Telegram is still your main channel, and everything you send
here also lands in the Buzz hub.

**Ask it to**, **Never send in chat**, and **Sending documents**: same as your
Telegram card.

**If it doesn't answer**

WhatsApp runs on an unofficial bridge and can go down. Wait 5 minutes, then
ask the same question on Telegram. Still stuck? Contact ________ (admin).

## SMS (extra channel)

**Your assistant:** save ________ as a contact named "Assistant" and text it.
Keep messages short; long replies may arrive in several parts. Telegram is
still your main channel.

**Ask it to**: same as the Telegram card.

**Never send by text**

- Anything on the restricted list: ________
- Passwords, bank details, ID numbers, health information
- SMS is not encrypted. If in doubt, don't text it.
